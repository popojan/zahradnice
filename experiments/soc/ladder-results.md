# forest-ladder: route-3 milestone 2 — the heritable trait ladder (2026-08-21)

Question (from evo-results.md verdict): upgrade the binary c/d
competition to a quantitative trait and ask whether the population's
mean trait *tracks* the environment. Design: 5 rungs (glyphs 1–5) on a
growth↔flammability trade-off (growth weight 6→1, fire-transmission
weight 4→0.25, mortality rent 0→0.01 rising toward the resistant end),
**heredity with mutation**: vegetative growth begets the parent's rung
at 0.9 of its rate, adjacent rungs at 0.05 each (edges reflect into
self, so total growth rate is position-independent). Environment =
lightning period P in the input mix (drive-in-quiescence, POC lesson
2). Generated by `gen_ladder.py` (88 rules); no engine changes.

Instrumentation: the trace's `src_line` field uniquely identifies each
rule, so `ladder_stats.py` replays a trace into exact per-rung
populations — verified glyph-for-glyph against final screen dumps in
all 80 runs (after two fixes: count initial `^` symbols; skip the
status row, whose `pop=`/`steps=` digits and right-aligned rule head
pollute naive glyph counts). Sweep harness `laddersweep.sh`
(process-parallel, per-run series CSVs in `ladder/`).

## Headline: tracking is real, but the tracker is an oscillator

Final-state statistics are the wrong observable — per-seed finals at
P=32000 range 1.21..4.60. The trajectories explain why: the system is
a **driven relaxation oscillator on the trait axis**, and finals are
phase samples. The honest statistic is the time-averaged mean rung
(second half of run), which is monotone in fire frequency:

| condition | P (events) | evt-avg meanrung | frozen input-time |
|---|---|---|---|
| fiery | 200 | 4.36 [4.32..4.39] | 0 |
| mid | 1000 | 4.16 [4.09..4.26] | <0.7% (bound) |
| calm | 4000 | 4.10 [4.02..4.23] | <1.3% (bound) |
| p12000 | 12000 | 3.75 [3.57..3.91] | 3.9–6.2% where measurable |
| p32000 | 32000 | 3.58 [3.53..3.69] | **13.9%** (exact, all seeds) |
| none | ∞ | 1.00 (absorbed by ~6.3k events) | ~96% |

(8 seeds each, 150k events for P≤4000, 300k for P≥12000, 33×64
field. Counting frozen time at meanrung 1.0 makes the gradient
steeper: p32000 ≈ 3.2 in input time.)

## The cycle, quantitatively

- **Ascent is an event**: a strike on a flammable-heavy field is a
  megafire; survivors skew resistant; post-fire regrowth starts at
  meanrung ≈ 4.4.
- **Descent is succession**: at saturation the only applicable rules
  are mortality, so rent — nominally weight 0.0025–0.01 — fires at
  certainty (the POC's rarity-vs-applicable-mass lesson, now on the
  mortality side, working *for* us). Vacancies at patch boundaries go
  to faster growers 4:1; pioneers invade by interface motion plus the
  5→1 mutation chain. Descent 4.4 → ~2.5 takes ~30k events; descent
  to **rung-1 fixation** ~30–60k post-fire (~6k from a mixed start —
  the `none` control).
- **The bottom is frozen**: all-1 saturation has no `.`, and rung 1
  pays no rent — *nothing is applicable*. The world stops consuming
  event time and waits for lightning. Located via strike-schedule
  drift in the trace: consecutive strikes 3 events apart bracket a
  32000-char frozen interval. ~6% of strikes on the frozen field
  fizzle (burnout wins the first jump vs 16:1 transmission mass) and
  the world refreezes for a whole extra period.
- Regime classification is P vs τ_descent: P ≪ τ pins the ladder
  resistant; P ≈ τ (12k) wanders in mid-ladder; P > τ (32k) is a
  full-amplitude limit cycle touching frozen fixation; P = ∞ absorbs.

## Secondary results

- **Mutation-rate test** (MUT 0.1→0.3): calm average drops 4.10→3.93,
  fiery unchanged (4.36→4.34). Descent is supply-limited at the
  margin, but the binding constraint is the strike interval itself —
  tripling mutation does not unpin the P≤4000 regimes.
- **Switch runs** (75k+75k): c2f is already high at the switch (calm
  is pinned); f2c drifts down only −0.2 mean in 75k calm events —
  adaptation is strike-fast upward, succession-slow downward. The
  ratchet of POC lesson 1, now with a timescale.
- **Trait resolution changes the outcome**: the 2-species POC's calm
  regime was contested/bistable (c up to 54%); the ladder's calm
  regime is pinned resistant. With intermediates available and
  mutation to move on them, the population climbs the smooth fitness
  landscape and holds (adaptive-dynamics-style ESS climb); the
  two-extremes world had to gamble whole-population bets. A
  discretization choice is an ecological force.
- Determinism replication: calm and c2f share bit-identical first
  halves (same seed, same input prefix) — prefix-replay invariance
  across condition files.

## Route-3 reading

The milestone asked for "species as weight rungs" — delivered, and it
answered with dynamics richer than the equilibrium-tracking question:
selection acting on state tunes the effective parameter, but the
tuning is a limit cycle whose time-average tracks the environment
monotonically. No optimizer, no new engine features; the descent-time
constant τ ≈ 30–60k events is an emergent property of interface
succession + mutation supply, not anything written in the rules.

## Interpretation (post-session discussion, same day)

**The oscillator is the correct answer, not a caveat.** "Does the
mean trait tune to the environment" quietly assumed an equilibrium
tracker; but the environment here is a pulse train, instantaneous
selection is directional in *both* phases (up-ladder in fire,
down-ladder in peace), and nothing stabilizes intermediate rungs on
their own — rung 3 is instantaneously inferior to 1 in peace and to 5
in fire. Under those conditions no interior fixed point is possible;
the only steady behaviours are pinned-at-an-end or orbiting, and
which one appears is set by P/τ_descent. Control-theory frame: the
sweep measured the population's *transfer function* — τ_descent is
its bandwidth; fiery (P ≪ τ) sits above the cutoff and feels only the
time-averaged environment (equilibrium-like, tight ranges), p32000
(P > τ) resolves each phase and swings full amplitude. This is
textbook fire-succession / fluctuating-selection dynamics, reached
from the substrate's own rules — evidence *for* the
mechanism-microscope claim. The methodological consequence stands:
finals are phase samples; report cycle average, amplitude, period.

**What it diagnoses for route 3: the missing ingredient is memory.**
The population responds to selection but retains nothing — everything
fire builds leaks away at 1/τ_descent, and τ is set by the ecology
(interface succession, mutation supply), not by anything chosen.
Learning in the RL sense is precisely a slow variable that integrates
the reward signal faster than it leaks. The ladder has no such
variable: composition is simultaneously the policy and the memory.
Sharpened route-3 spec: **engineer a state variable whose retention
time is decoupled from the environment's fluctuation time.**

**The integrator belongs in the derivation, not the rules.** Rules
stay immutable — laws of physics; all adaptation lives in
configuration, exactly as in chemistry/biology (fixed local laws,
unbounded adaptation in concentrations; DNA is the existence proof
that arbitrarily long retention is expressible as matter under fixed
laws). The general mechanism is **mass-action encoding**: the sampler
selects proportional to applicable mass, so the *abundance* of a
token species is an effective rate dial — "mutable weights" without
mutating any weight. Available slow-variable constructions, all
in-grammar: dormancy/seed bank (tokens most rules cannot touch, rare
germination), copy-number counters, spatial refugia, bistable motifs
(attractor = bit), the per-cell memory struct. One subtlety is
jump-chain time: there is no absolute clock, so "slow" must be
manufactured as a *ratio* against a persistent fast process — rarity
is relative to co-applicable mass (this session's frozen worlds are
the cautionary demonstration). And reward-coupling needs no global
signal read: a rule that scores can deposit the memory token *in the
same rewrite* — correlation exact by construction.

## Open next (milestone 3, revised)

Build the in-derivation integrator first: a seed-bank layer whose
retention time is demonstrably tunable over orders of magnitude and
decoupled from P — it converts the oscillator into a low-pass filter
with memory and is the prerequisite for any learning claim.
Encoding draft: **pair cells** (1×2, `#grid 2 1`, the full-block
idiom — also the aesthetically square pixel): left char = standing
phenotype, right char = co-located seed-bank slot; fire touches left
only, deposits write right, rare germination copies right back to
left. Alternative: Unicode product states (e.g. Braille U+2800–FF as
a visible 8-bit field per cell). Only chars are matchable — colours
and `$`-memory are display/restore channels — so compute-state must
be spelled in glyphs; the generator absorbs the product rule count.
Reward-correlated capacities come after (they need memory to be worth
anything). Score-conditioned rules remain the unbuilt engine option,
now reframed: a global observable added to fixed laws (a mean field),
not rule mutation — and possibly unnecessary given
deposit-with-reward. Larger-L clean flip is subsumed — the graded
response replaced the flip question.
