# forest-bank: route-3 milestone 3a — the seed-bank integrator (2026-08-21)

Question (ladder-results.md, revised next): build an in-derivation
slow variable — rules immutable, memory as matter — and show its
retention time is set by rule-weight ratios, not by the environment's
fluctuation. Design: dormant seeds as first-class field cells in
Braille (⠁⠃⠇⠏⠟, dot count = rung; mixed-field encoding, pair cells
deferred). Three rules per rung: deposit ('.' beside standing k →
seed k, true-breeding, weight DEP·w_k, DEP=0.02), germinate (seed →
standing k, weight G), decay (seed → '.', weight D). Fire and growth
have no rules touching seeds — protection by omission is physics.
Generated by `gen_bank.py`; slow bank G=2e-4/D=5e-5, fast bank ×30,
control DEP=0 (deposit rules absent — weight 0 would clamp to 1).

One deliberate physics change vs forest-ladder.cfg: rung 1 pays
mortality M1=0.001 (was 0). This guarantees a *coverage property*:
every cell type ('.', rung, A, seed) always has an applicable T-rule,
so T no-ops are impossible in any reachable state — event time ≡
input time (verified: 54/54 runs, events == input length exactly).
Without it, quiescent states renormalize the bank's tiny hazards to
certainty and the retention constant is undefined. This is designed,
not emergent: bankoff (same M1) is the only valid control; round-11
numbers are cross-physics context.

Instrumentation: ladder_stats.py extended with exact bank accounting
(trace-replayed standing + bank populations ≡ final screen glyph
counts in 54/54 runs). The Braille glyphs exposed an engine bug —
wide file streams (--dump-screen, F12 screenshots, check's reader)
used the classic-locale codecvt and silently produced empty files on
the first non-Latin-1 char; fixed with a hand-rolled UTF-8 facet
(src/wide_io.h — <codecvt> is removed in C++20), LC_NUMERIC pinned
to C in all mains (latent %lf comma-locale bug), engine behavior
verified bit-identical.

## E1 — the bank under the oscillator (P=32000, 6 seeds, 300k)

| variant | standing amp (sd) | bank size | bank amp (sd) |
|---|---|---|---|
| bankoff | 1.13 | — | — |
| bankslow | 1.17 | ~397 | **0.32** |
| bankfast | 1.33 | ~58 | 0.95 |

The bank does NOT damp the standing oscillation (no feedback), and it
was wrong to expect it to: germination flux is pinned far below
growth. What it does is **record**: the slow bank's own composition
is a low-pass copy of the driven trait (amplitude 0.32 vs 1.06,
hovering at the cycle mean), and the filter constant follows G+D —
the fast bank tracks (0.95), the slow bank integrates.

**Why no feedback is possible from this design (the destructive-
readout identity):** germination is the bank's only output and it
consumes the seed, so per seed, influence ≡ loss. For a bank of N:
influence/event × retention = N·G/(G+D) ≤ N, independent of G. Worse,
at queue equilibrium (Little's law: N* = deposit flux × residence),
germination flux = fidelity × deposit flux — G controls only the
delay, never the throughput; the bank can never shout louder than the
deposit whisper (measured: deposits 2.1% of growth events, fidelity
0.79 vs theory 0.80). Escapes, not built: catalytic readout (seed as
matched-but-unrewritten context boosting adjacent sprouting —
read-by-template, i.e. transcription, vs read-by-consumption;
~20 rules), or fire-cued germination (spend the fixed budget at
maximum leverage — serotiny). The living population already IS the
catalytic hot tier (a plant emits copies without being consumed);
the bank is the cold tier: hot steers, cold remembers. Nature runs
both; asking the archive to be a thermostat was E1's over-ask.

**Field note (interactive, 325k steps, user's F12 capture of the
-cat variant):** a single frame from a long live run caught both
memory tiers at once — standing crop 1321 cells at meanrung 2.32
(63% pioneers, mid-descent), bank 662 seeds = 33% of the field at
bankmean 2.82: the cold archive still remembering a fierier past
than the crop it lags. **Zero fire, exactly one bare cell** — and
this is the *catalytic* variant, which refines the firestorm story:
fire-free stretches exist even here, because the catalytic channel
is gap-gated (a packed world silences template sprouting entirely)
and the fuel loop needs bank *consensus* on the flammable end —
this bank is mixed (majority share 27%), so sprouts entering gaps
carry mostly low-flammability rungs and fires die normally. The
self-sustaining fire is a *state* (consensus + open gaps), not a
property of the rule set. Interactive timing (`l` every 3 s
wall-clock vs immediate T) also puts live runs deep in the P ≫ τ
regime, so calm is the norm and fire a rare pulse. (The capture
also first-verified today's UTF-8 screenshot fix on the interactive
path — and exposed that all variants shared the `#!` status name;
gen_bank.py now stamps the variant into the status line.)

## E2 — behavioral retention (A: none 40k → B: fiery ∈ {15k, 60k,
240k} → C: none 120k; t50 = events into C until share1 ≥ 0.5)

Phase A stores a rung-1 world (and, in bank arms, a rung-1 archive);
phase B adversarially overwrites the standing crop (resistant, mr@C ≈
4.0–4.4 in all arms); phase C measures recovery. 4 seeds/arm, medians:

| B | bankoff | bankfast | bankslow | slow's bank1@C |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 15k | 45.5k† | 35.5k† | **30k†** | 146–158 |
| 60k | 27k | 23.5k | **15k** | 119–134 |
| 240k | 24.5k | 25k | **13.5k** | 53–67 |

† B=15k has a shared ~15k refill confound: the first strikes wipe the
all-1 continent and 15k of fiery hasn't rebuilt it (pop@C ≈ 950 vs
~2020), so t50 = refill + descent for every arm.

Findings:
1. **Memory is behaviorally retrievable.** The slow bank halves
   recovery time (27k→15k, 24.5k→13.5k). Mechanism visible in the
   archive column: surviving rung-1 seeds germinate into pioneers
   that nucleate patches which the hot tier then amplifies — even 54
   remaining seeds (7% of a bank flooded with recent resistant
   deposits) suffice. Absolute archive size matters, not purity.
2. **Retention is weight-ratio-set and spans the designed range.**
   The fast bank's archive survives B=15k (bank1@C 15–35, advantage
   −10k) and is dead by B=60k (bank1@C ≈ 0, advantage ≈ 0) — its
   retention sits between 15k and 60k of fiery time. The slow
   archive decays 150 → 60 across 225k of fiery (half-life ≈ 170k
   events, ~1200 fire cycles) and still delivers −11k at B=240k. A
   ×30 weight ratio produced the ordered ×10–30 retention separation.
3. **Population-only heredity retains nothing** (the "is DNA in the
   living population sufficient?" control): bankoff re-derives rung 1
   from scratch by mutation+selection at τ_descent ≈ 25–30k
   regardless of history. Hot memory is erased by the environment
   that selects it; the store and the exposure are the same object.
   Nature's verdict matches: dormancy evolved everywhere disturbance
   is catastrophic — the bank is not machinery beside biology, it is
   a life stage (the population extended in time).
4. **Memory also makes adaptation reproducible**: bankslow's t50
   spread at B=240k is 13–15k vs bankoff's 21–33k — nucleation from
   an archive replaces waiting for lucky mutants (evolutionary rescue
   from standing variation vs de novo), cutting variance ~5×.
5. **Λ-renormalization measured as physics**: the fast bank survives
   15k of *fiery* time (huge applicable mass shields seeds — fire
   freezes bank time) though it would not survive 15k of quiet. The
   retention "constant" is a ratio to ambient mass, as the jump-chain
   caveat predicted.
6. Honest limit: the slow advantage fades only mildly with B
   (−15.5k → −11k), because alongside phase-A memory the bank
   continuously archives pioneer transients during B (steady-state
   channel). The pure phase-A signal is the bank1@C decay curve plus
   the fading component; separating them cleanly would need a
   deposit-free phase B (a DEP gate), which the current design can't
   express without a second cfg.

## E3 — catalytic readout (forest-bank-cat.cfg, +20 rules: a seed
templates its rung into adjacent ground without being consumed)

Predictions were: feedback appears (E1 damping succeeds), retention
survives being read. Both wrong, both instructively (18/18 runs
reconcile exactly):

1. **Retention became unbounded — the memory is alive.** Recovery
   after adversity: t50 = 6.5k (B=60k) and 3k (B=240k) vs passive
   bank's 15k/13.5k and control's 27k/24.5k; share1 goes 0.17→0.86
   in ~10k events once fire stops. And bank1@C *rises* with B
   (172→246): the loop seed→sprout→deposit→seed regenerates the
   archive faster than G+D drain it. Catalytic readout converts the
   cold store into a self-renewing lineage occupying a
   dormancy+expression life cycle — no longer a record *of* the
   population but a replicator population itself.
2. **Feedback destabilizes instead of damping** (standing amplitude
   1.32 > passive 1.17 > control 1.13 at P=32000). The bank feeds
   back a *lagged* low-pass copy of the trait; delayed positive
   feedback under periodic driving is phase-wrong — damping would
   need negative or phase-corrected feedback, which no pure archive
   can supply. (Control-theory truism, rediscovered the hard way.)
3. **Niche construction, uninvited**: at B=15k the rung-1-consensus
   bank under fire feeds flammable sprouts into burning gaps and the
   fire becomes self-sustaining *without lightning* (≈800 burning
   cells for 14k+ events after strikes end; t50=44k is fire
   persistence, not memory failure). This is the pyrogenic-vegetation
   syndrome — fireproof seed bank + flammable expression, fire wielded
   against competitors (the eucalyptus strategy) — emerging from the
   rule set, not designed into it.
4. **Consensus register**: bank majority share 0.64 vs passive 0.40 —
   autocatalysis makes the archive winner-take-all: robust one-value
   memory, unfaithful analog record. Memory fidelity and memory
   robustness traded off through the same loop gain.

### The fire that would not die (plain-language walkthrough)

Step by step, for the reader who skipped the tables. Normally fire is
an *event*: a burning cell either spreads (needs a flammable
neighbour) or burns out (weight 1), and once the fuel around it is
gone, burnout is all that's left — the fire dies in a handful of
events. Now add a catalytic seed bank that has drifted to consensus
on species 1, the fastest and most flammable:

1. Seeds are fireproof — no fire rule touches them. Under the ash,
   the archive survives untouched.
2. The catalytic rule lets a seed sprout its species into adjacent
   bare ground *without being spent*.
3. A fire produces exactly what a seed needs: bare ground, right next
   to still-burning cells.
4. The bank instantly resprouts species 1 into those gaps.
5. A burning neighbour ignites the fresh sprout almost surely
   (transmission weight 4 against burnout weight 1).
6. The new burning cell clears more ground beside more seeds — back
   to step 4.

Burnout removes burning cells; the sprout-and-ignite loop resupplies
them at nearly the same rate. Measured: ~800 burning cells sustained
for 14,000+ events *after the last lightning strike* — the fire burns
with no ignition source at all. Fire has stopped being an event and
become a state: a standing fire climax fed by the ecosystem itself.

The evolutionary logic closes when you notice the *other* thing that
never dies: the seed consensus — the same loop seen from the other
side. Fire cannot touch the seeds, but it reliably kills the standing
competition (species 2–5), and every burnt gap is territory only the
bank can colonize instantly. The sprouts' flammability is not a
defect; it is a weapon. The lineage keeps the fire alive because the
fire clears space, while its genome lies fireproof in the soil. Real
ecology knows this as pyrogenic vegetation — the eucalyptus
fire-embracing syndrome. None of it was designed: it emerged from
twenty template-sprouting rules.

To watch it: `./zahradnice experiments/soc/forest-bank-cat.cfg`,
throw lightning with `l` into the green consensus, and wait for the
fire that refuses to go out.

And when it *doesn't* happen — long fire-free stretches occur even
in this variant (see the field note above) — the loop is missing one
of its two preconditions: the bank must hold a **consensus on the
flammable end** (a mixed archive sprouts mostly slow-burning rungs,
and the fire's reproduction number drops below one), and there must
be **open gaps** (in a fully packed world the catalytic channel is
silent, because template sprouting needs adjacent bare ground). The
eternal fire is a state the system visits, not a fate.

The destructive-readout identity thus splits into a triad: consumptive
readout = faithful, bounded-retention, influence-capped (bankslow);
catalytic readout = self-renewing, unbounded-retention,
consensus-forming, dynamically destabilizing (bankcat); no readout =
no memory (bankoff). Nature uses all three tiers plus regulation on
the loop gain — which is presumably where "learning" hides: a
reward-coupled *gate* on the catalytic channel.

## Milestone verdict

The integrator exists, in-derivation, rules immutable: retention
tunable via G+D over the designed range, decoupled from the fire
period (same weights filter at P=32000 and retain across P=200
epochs), read out behaviorally, with exact accounting throughout.
The two-tier architecture (hot catalytic population + cold protected
archive) reproduces nature's memory design and dissolves E1's
damping expectation into the destructive-readout identity; E3 then
showed the third tier and its price (self-renewal, consensus,
pyrogenic niche construction). Next rungs: reward-coupled gating of
the catalytic channel (the loop-gain regulator — the natural home of
the learning claim), fire-cued germination (conditional readout),
hidden-genome channel in living cells (pair-cell recessive
reservoir — milestone 3b).
