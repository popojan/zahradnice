# Forest experiments: from sandpiles to living memory

A story arc in four documents, each building on the last (all runs on
the stock engine; programs are `.cfg` grammars, most generated by the
`gen_*.py` scripts beside them):

1. **results.md** — self-organized criticality probes: Drossel–Schwabl
   forest fire (`forest.cfg`, `forest2`, `forest3`) shows
   parameter-dependent megafire regimes rather than SOC; the Manna
   sandpile (`manna.cfg`, `gen_manna.py`) passes L-scaling and
   self-organizes its density. Calibration of the instrument.
2. **evo-results.md** — `forest-evo.cfg`: two species on a
   growth↔flammability trade-off under calm vs fiery lightning.
   Directional selection demonstrated; five structural lessons
   (seed rain, mortality rent, environment as input mix).
3. **ladder-results.md** — `forest-ladder.cfg` (`gen_ladder.py`):
   the trade-off becomes a 5-rung heritable trait with mutation.
   The mean trait tracks fire frequency — but as a driven relaxation
   oscillator, not an equilibrium; retention of anything is the
   missing ingredient.
4. **bank-results.md** — `forest-bank*.cfg` (`gen_bank.py`): dormant
   seeds in Braille glyphs as an in-world memory. Passive banks
   retain and halve re-adaptation time; catalytic (read-by-template)
   banks become self-renewing lineages, destabilize the oscillation,
   and — see "The fire that would not die" — invent pyrogenic niche
   construction on their own.

5. **../bandit/bandit-results.md** — the summit: a two-armed bandit
   in 25 rules. Policy = token masses, learning = deposit in the
   scoring rewrite, forgetting = decay. Learns in 2k events, reverses
   reliably, matches jump-chain capture theory, and rediscovers the
   condition for learning to be adaptive (environment must outlive
   the relearning time). Route 3's founding question: answered yes.

6. **../garden/garden-results.md** — the gardener's own experiment:
   three species differing only in learning capability compete for
   ground. Cheap errors select for activity over accuracy (a busy
   wrong learner outbreeds a random one); expensive errors make
   punishment king, and its value grows with environmental change.
   Selection over learning architectures, no fitness function
   anywhere.

Watch any of them live: `./zahradnice experiments/soc/<file>.cfg`
(`l` throws lightning, `q` quits; the Braille dots are sleeping
seeds). Analysis tools: `ladder_stats.py` (exact trace-to-screen
accounting), `laddersweep.sh` (regime sweeps), `bank_probe.py`,
`ladder_agg.py`, `firestats.py`, `avalanche.py`.
