# Inverse emergence, night 13: walls as paid matter — containment works when it makes the parasite unexpressible, and the medicine is mutagenic

Night 11's verdict stood: free compartments (rows) never suppress
the order-fueled parasite, and at collapse they feed it. Its
honest-limits section named the follow-up: walls as paid matter —
containment priced in the world's own currencies. Night 13 ran it.
Driver `night13.py`, data `night13_walls.csv`; chem {plain,
paraonly} × walls {0, 2, 4, 8} × m {8, 2} × 24 seeds = 384 runs,
all exact, 10 s wall. Predictions P13-1..4 registered in the
driver docstring before running.

A wall `|` is in-row matter: it displaces one gene of capacity,
taxes every patrol crossing (arrive/settle/exit chain, three
events against f's one; wall preserved behind the head, toll paid
from the one A3 budget — 1.0k/2.0k/3.7k events at w2/w4/w8, up to
15% of the run), and blocks every pattern-window rule across it
(`|` matches no gene). A head exiting onto a post-wall hole
repairs it blind (50/50) — walls are miscopy hotspots by
construction. Walls are permanent this night; erosion and
wall-repair economics remain the registered follow-up.

## Findings

**1. The parasite's activity falls monotonically with walls, to
exactly zero.** Deceptive-pair events per run, paraonly: m=8:
204 → 166 → 112 → **0**; m=2: 1138 → 598 → 271 → **0** across
w0/2/4/8. At sector width 2 the deceptive gapwrite's expression
pattern (same-glyph west + double gap) physically does not fit
between walls: the parasite is not out-competed or diluted — its
niche is made geometrically unexpressible. This is the EW-3
scarcity law of the earned-channel arc in pattern space:
containment works exactly insofar as it makes the parasite's fuel
(runs and gap pairs) locally impossible, which free rows — full
rings where the pattern fits fine — never touched.

**2. At collapse, the night-11 inversion REVERSES (P13-2 held,
strongly).** m=2 paraonly ρ: 0.162 (w0) → 0.083 → 0.069 →
0.000 (w8, t=−4.1) — a dose-response suppression where free rows
gave +0.054 elevation (t≈8.4). Caveat reported: at m=2 the tape
is nearly dead everywhere (0.15–0.19 of capacity; 12/24 w8 runs
end with no gene pairs at all, and ρ means run over the valid
remainder) — the unconditional claim is the activity ledger
(zero deceptive events), and the conditional one is that the
REMNANT is crystal in walled worlds and glass in open ones.
Walls do not save the tape at m=2 — nothing does — they decide
what pattern the survivors carry. The wall preserves the
pattern, not the matter.

**3. P13-1 failed as stated — and the failure isolates the
medicine's side effect.** At m=8, paraonly ρ is FLAT in walls
(0.234 / 0.224 / 0.214 / 0.224, |t| ≤ 0.8) even though parasite
activity falls to zero. The decomposition is the plain-chemistry
arm: in a healthy world, walls INJECT disorder — ρ 0.002 (w0) →
0.095 → 0.126 → 0.224 (w8), t ≈ +6 — entirely from the
blind-repair flanks (a head exiting a wall onto a wound hole
reseeds it by coin flip). Wall-injected disorder at w8 (0.224)
almost exactly replaces the parasite's own (0.234): the ρ
metric was the wrong registered instrument, the event ledger the
right one, and the substituted disorder is the price P13-4
pointed at (the direct flank-vs-interior split within paraonly
is underpowered, t=+0.6; the plain arms measure the flank effect
at full strength instead).

**4. P13-3 held: containment is insurance, and the premium is
itemized.** In plain chemistry walls are pure cost: capacity
(tape ceiling down by walls/24), toll (up to 15% of all events at
w8), and mutagenic flanks (ρ up two orders of magnitude from
0.002). Nothing is bought — there is nothing to contain. The
wall pays only in the epidemic, and even there it pays in
pattern, not in matter.

## Cross-arc synthesis

Night 11 and night 13 together sharpen what "compartment" must
mean for the classical remedy to work in this substrate: not
group structure (rows changed selection's bookkeeping and the
parasite thrived), but LOCAL NICHE DESTRUCTION (walls changed
what patterns can exist, and the parasite vanished). The
earned-channel arc's E4/E5 said abundance neutralizes a channel
and scarcity selects on it; night 13 adds the containment
corollary: a barrier matters exactly insofar as it makes some
resource — here, the parasite's expression window — locally
scarce to the point of impossibility. And every benefit was
priced: events, capacity, and a new mutational load at the
boundaries, the substrate's own version of a medicine with side
effects.

## Honest limits & hooks

- Walls are permanent and free to CREATE (one bootstrap shot);
  wall EROSION under pokes, wall-repair rules, and
  builder-lineages that pay for their own walls (the full
  autopoietic boundary, Ono–Ikegami) remain open — this night
  priced standing walls, not wall ownership.
- The blind-repair flank is a design choice (the exiting head
  has no covered-gene memory); a wall chemistry with
  gene-memory crossing states would trade alphabet for fidelity
  — the alphabet-as-price doctrine, unexplored here.
- m=2 conditional claims are survivor-conditioned as stated
  above; m=8 carries the full-power unconditional ledger.
- Sector width 2 is the geometric extinction point for THIS
  parasite's window; a parasite with a smaller window would need
  denser walls — the window-size vs wall-density phase boundary
  is one sweep away.
