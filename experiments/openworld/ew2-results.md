# EW-2 — the fuel economy of law-copying (F4, earning the price)

Date: 2026-08-23, session 9. Registrations: ew-design.md §EW-2
(P2-2', P2-3', Amendments 5–6, all pre-sweep). Driver: ew2.py;
data: ew2_runs.csv (800 runs, zero exactness failures). Verdict up
front: **the fuel price of law-copying is earned** — no fuel, no
inheritance, anywhere, ever; and WHERE fuel lands decides, exactly,
where law can be copied. Plus two structural findings the sweep
forced: the pass rule (and mortality as jam-clearing), and the
pursuit endgame.

## Setup

5 playfield rows (tape / marker / fuel / machinery / regulatory);
uniform-α ring 24; ETERNAL machinery density 1/2 (demography was
EW-3's business — this isolates the economy); copy consumes one
positional fuel token `o` above the copier, restored to the `.`
background; walks and passes free. Static half-ring markers (`<`
cols 0–11, `>` 12–23) anchor feed rules that drop `o` under a
random marker of their half — placement is pure drive composition
(f/g bytes; O9's instrument aimed at the inheritance channel).
Drive: b + (T C⁴)*100 + blocks × (p + feed + (T C⁴)*10), feed = 2
tokens/wound. Arms: starve/left/right/both × horizons 60 and 150
blocks × 100 seeds.

## The two structural findings (amendments 5–6, both pre-sweep)

1. **The pass rule / mortality as jam-clearing.** With eternal
   machinery the first smoke DEADLOCKED: a wound repair can
   re-de-regulate the gate under a standing copier, which then can
   neither copy (no template) nor walk (stamped-target guard);
   east-only exclusion jams the entire convoy behind it. EW-3/EW-4
   never saw this because their copiers decay — mortal machinery
   melts traffic jams, an unregistered service of death. Fix:
   copiers may PASS over an unstamped locus without stamping it.
2. **The pursuit endgame.** East-only regrowth on a ring makes a
   dead arc a pursuer, not a border: wounds at the fed half's west
   edge cannot be repaired (dead west neighbour), so the death
   frontier advances at the wound rate and laps the ring. The
   alive-contrast of steering is transient by construction.

- **P2-2' HELD exactly**: the starve arm performed ZERO copies in
  200 runs at both horizons — with no fuel the inheritance channel
  simply does not exist, and the world erodes to death.
- **P2-3' HELD exactly on steering**: across 400 single-fed runs
  the unfed half's copy count is 0.0 — not low, zero (a copy can
  only anchor at a fueled column; the registered ≤1-cell stamp leak
  is the only cross-boundary effect). At the 60-block horizon the
  fed half is alive and active while the unfed half approaches the
  starve arm (left arm: active 6.0 vs 1.0; mirrored in the right
  arm; both-arm symmetric).
- **The pursuit, as re-registered**: at 150 blocks both single-fed
  arms are fully dead — fed half included, fuel still standing
  (11.9 tokens unspent over a corpse) — while the both-arm world
  persists (alive 15.8/24). A law economy cannot buy survival with
  local wealth if regrowth is one-handed and its neighbourhood is
  dead; the granary-graveyard motif (OW-7's two carrion traps) at
  the level of the inheritance channel itself.
