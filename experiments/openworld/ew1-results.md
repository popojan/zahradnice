## The two design collisions (both hit in smoke runs, both
registered as amendments BEFORE the sweep)

1. **Conquest = content change.** The naive axiom "creation
   de-regulates the written column" let F2's idempotent spawner
   re-de-regulate the locus a copier had just stamped, forever; law
   thrashed in every spawner world. Refinement: de-regulate exactly
   the cells whose matter CONTENT changes (matter is anonymous —
   rewriting A over A is no identity event). Expressed by splitting
   the unconditional write kind into three mutually exclusive
   east-content variants; exactly one applies in any state at the
   rule's own weight — lottery-equivalent to F3's single rule.
2. **Machinery needs its own trigger byte.** On a shared T, copier
   events starve the matter event budget and the night-2 classifier
   (ABSORBED = fewer events than budget) reads every earned world as
   frozen. Copy/walk moved to byte C (house drive-composition
   pattern); machinery bandwidth became the drive's T:C cadence — an
   explicit, sweepable resource.
