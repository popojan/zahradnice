# EW-9 — heritable machine-lineage fidelity (F4)

Date: 2026-08-24, session 12. Registrations: ew-design.md §EW-9
(P9-1..3, Amendments 9–10, all pre-sweep). Driver: ew9.py; data:
ew9_runs.csv (300 runs, zero exactness failures). Verdict up
front: **fidelity is now a heritable machine-lineage trait, and
selection acts on it — asymmetrically. A lineage is selected
AGAINST deleterious error but not FOR beneficial error, because
in this substrate error's costs are private and error's gifts are
public.**

## Setup

EW-5 geometry minus code/translation: machinery demography is
replication − decay only. Faithful Π and sloppy π (ε = 0.2 per
copy, mass-normalized per Amendment 10) breed true from a 6+6
seed; replication SPLITS a machine east into an empty slot, gated
on standing over living law, weighted by that allele's
machine-nurture (Amendment 9: all mutant targets carry α's exact
matter law and differ only in nurture — d = 0, ν = 1, μ = 2 —
one axis, no matter-law confounds). Two instrument findings en
route, registered as amendments: selection needs ~20 mutation
events per run to beat drift (ε raised to 0.2), and the naive
sloppy machine's extra headers made it MOVE more and hence breed
less (exclusion queuing) — the mobility–fecundity confound that
tilted even the neutral arm until copy mass was normalized.

## Results (100 seeds/arm; share = faithful fraction of standing
machinery)

| arm | survived | Π-share | births Π/π | miscopies | target land |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| neutral (ν, nurture 1) | 99/100 | 0.499 ± 0.357 | 41/40 | 18.3 | 9.8 |
| deleterious (d, nurture 0) | 47/100 | **0.577** ± 0.325 | 44/34 | 12.5 | 2.1 |
| beneficial (μ, nurture 2) | 96/100 | 0.492 ± 0.339 | 49/50 | 18.5 | 9.7 |

- **P9-1 HELD**: the neutral arm drifts dead-centered (z ≈ 0
  against 0.5) — after normalization the lineages are exactly
  exchangeable when errors have no consequences. The pre-fix
  neutral tilt (0.63) was the mobility confound, not selection.
- **P9-2 HELD (modest, real)**: with dead-law errors the faithful
  lineage wins (0.577, z ≈ +2.4; birth ledger 44 vs 34), and the
  world pays too — mutation load kills half the worlds (47/100
  survive vs 99 neutral). The selective handle is IMMEDIATE and
  PRIVATE: a copy advances the machine onto the cell it just
  stamped, so a miscopier stands on its own barren ground —
  unable to breed there and then. Its cost is delivered to its
  maker before mixing can socialize it.
- **P9-3 FAILED — and the failure is the law**: the sloppy
  lineage gains nothing from planting rich pasture (0.492,
  indistinguishable from neutral drift), even though its errors
  created ~40% of the ring's double-nurture land (9.7 μ-gates
  standing, 18.5 miscopies). The pasture outlives the planter's
  visit: machines of both lineages drift across the ring many
  times per run, so the nurture-2 benefit is grazed by everyone —
  a public good — while the one private breeding opportunity (the
  moment of standing on fresh μ) is negligible against a lifetime
  of shared grazing. **Error's costs are private; error's gifts
  are public.** Fidelity evolves upward easily and downward
  hardly: the substrate's derivation of why mutator lineages are
  transient hitchhikers and why selection FOR evolvability is
  indirect and weak (second-order selection), here separated from
  all confounds by construction — the two arms differ from
  neutral by one nurture number each, and only the private-cost
  arm moves the lineage share.

## What EW-9 closes

The last residue: fidelity now rides machine lineages (breeding
true, competing, selectable), completing the set — inheritance
(EW-1/3), mutation (EW-4), price (EW-1/2/3), description (EW-5/6),
code (EW-7/8), and now the mutation RATE itself as heritable
matter. The asymmetry law is the residue's replacement: what
remains open is not whether fidelity can be selected but the
regimes that could invert P9-3 — stronger viscosity (less mixing:
shorter runs, wider rings, slower machines), privatized pasture
(nurture gated on the creator's lineage — kin recognition, one
glyph away), or environments where μ-land's benefit is
excludable. Each is one registered arm away; all are named here
rather than run.

Honest limits: ε and the lineage glyphs remain authored (the
Π↔π switch — mutation of the mutation rate — is still off);
populations are small (6–16 machines) so drift is strong and the
deleterious effect, though directional, is modest; d-templates
were made copyable (the deleterious allele spreads by faithful
copying once created), which dilutes but does not erase the
private-cost channel.
