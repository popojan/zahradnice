# EW-10 — linkage, or what selection-for-evolvability needs (F4)

Date: 2026-08-24, session 12. Registrations: ew-design.md §EW-10
(P10-1..4, pre-run). Driver: ew10.py; data: ew10_runs.csv (600
runs, zero exactness failures). Verdict up front: **selection FOR
evolvability turns on exactly when the gift of error is made
excludable — recognitional linkage moves the lineage share ten
standard errors; spatial linkage alone drowns in the drift it
accelerates.** EW-9's asymmetry law is not a wall but a price
list, and the price is linkage.

## Setup

EW-9's world and constants (ε 0.2 mass-normalized, w_r 0.02,
nurture μ = 2). Linkage supplied in its two separable forms:
VISCOSITY (walk/pass weights 1 → 0.1: machines stay near their
birthplace and their plantings) and KIN pasture (the μ-nurture
replicate rule belongs to π only — full excludability; the
linkage is authored, and that is the point: the arm tests whether
linkage rescues second-order selection, not whether it
self-assembles). Invasion arms start all-faithful with a rare
symmetric fidelity switch Π↔π. 100 seeds/arm.

## Results (share = faithful fraction; EW-9 anchors: neutral
0.499, beneficial-public 0.492)

| arm | Π-share | births Π/π | survived |
|---|---|---|---|
| public | 0.492 ± 0.339 | 49/50 | 96 |
| viscous | 0.509 ± 0.488 | 334/371 | 100 |
| kin | **0.210 ± 0.291** | 28/64 | 95 |
| viscous-kin | 0.324 ± 0.470 | 250/446 | 99 |
| invade-public | 0.574 ± 0.205 | 70/24 | 97 |
| invade-kin | **0.400 ± 0.231** | 52/33 | 95 |

- **P10-1 HELD, decisively**: kin-gated pasture takes the sloppy
  lineage to a 79:21 standing majority (z ≈ −10 against drift;
  births 64 vs 28). The moment error's gifts are cotransmitted —
  here by recognition — the mutation rate becomes an ordinary
  selectable trait. The public arm replicates EW-9's null to
  three decimals (0.492), anchoring the contrast.
- **P10-2 FAILED — and the mechanism matters**: viscosity alone
  gives π nothing (0.509), because slowing movement freed lottery
  mass for demography: turnover rose ~8× (births 705 vs 99 per
  run), and acceleration speeds DRIFT as much as selection. The
  ±0.488 spread is near-total bimodality — lineages form solid
  1-D blocks whose boundaries random-walk to fixation within the
  run. In a small one-dimensional world, spatial linkage without
  recognition is self-defeating: the viscosity that preserves the
  lineage–pasture association also hands the outcome to drift.
  (A caveat for the spatial-cooperation literature's intuitions:
  pattern-formation rescues need room; ring 24 with blocks is
  not it.)
- **P10-3 FAILED with the same mechanism**: viscous-kin (0.324)
  is WEAKER for π than kin alone (0.210) — the drift acceleration
  partially undoes what recognition buys.
- **P10-4 HELD via its registered contrast**: from an all-faithful
  start the mutator lineage, created only by the switch, ends in
  the MAJORITY under kin (0.400 → 60% π) and in the minority
  under public (0.574), z ≈ 8 between arms. Caveat reported: the
  switch fired ~17 times per run rather than the intended ~5 (the
  applicable-mass arithmetic underestimated), so symmetric
  switching itself pushes shares toward 0.5; the between-arm
  contrast, not the absolute level, carries the invasion claim.

## The through-line, for the capstone

EW-10 closes a pattern the arc has now measured five times: a
trait or channel is selectable exactly when its consequences are
EXCLUDABLE, and drifts when they are a commons — machinery
funding (EW-3: abundance neutralizes, scarcity selects), rescue
alleles (EW-4: the rescuer's self-limiting advantage), the codon
table (EW-8: the frozen accident — a bad code's cost is global,
so drift decides), fidelity downward (EW-9: gifts are public),
and now its inversion (EW-10: privatize the gift and the mutator
invades). Containment's version is night 13 (a wall works by
making the parasite's niche locally impossible). One law wearing
six masks: **selection sees only what someone can keep.**

Honest limits: the kin channel is authored (a recognition glyph
away from earned — μ stamped by π would need to be a distinct
glyph whose nurture rule self-assembles; named, not built); the
switch rate overshot its design; viscous arms' drift-vs-selection
decomposition is inferred from variance shape, not per-event
lineage tracking; ring 24 and 12-machine populations keep drift
strong everywhere.
