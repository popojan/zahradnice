\centerline{\it Ten nights of inverse emergence: repair, heredity,
and parasitism in eleven rules}
\medskip

\noindent{\bf Abstract.}
How much of evolution must be written into physical law, and how
much is a property of matter? We attack the question by deletion.
The laboratory is a deliberately small physics --- five axioms, none
of which mentions copying, fitness, or lineage: local rewriting of
typed cells on a ring, one event at a time, under a fixed rule
table and an external damage source. Over ten experiments
(“nights”) we first {\it find} minimal mechanisms by exhaustively
running every small rule set in a declared family, then compose
them into evolving systems, then delete the authored ingredients one by one --- rate dials,
lineages, mutation operators, fitness functions, copy primitives.
The end state is a family of worlds of nine to thirteen uniform
rules exhibiting selection, heredity with a seed bank, adaptation
with an arrow, evolutionary rescue, error catastrophe, a commons
tragedy, and a parasite enforcing permanent coexistence and a
self-sustaining variation supply --- none of it named in any
rule. Six laws survive translation out of
the substrate; a mean-field reduction explains the sharpest of
them, accounts for two measured robustnesses, and its predicted
circulating probability current is measured directly ---
certifying the parasite phase as a genuine non-equilibrium steady
state. Every
quantitative claim is verified by exact accounting: independently
replaying each run's event log reconstructs its final state
byte-for-byte (about 26{,}000 runs, zero mismatches), at a total
compute cost of under an hour on a laptop.

\sec The question

Models of evolution usually begin by assuming its ingredients.
Population genetics assumes replicators and a fitness function;
genetic algorithms assume a selection loop; artificial-life systems
relax some assumptions and keep others. Tierra and Avida \cite[ray91,ofria04]
hand-author the first replicator and let selection emerge;
random program soups grow replicators with no fitness function,
but from copy-capable instruction sets \cite[aguera24]; the
evolving-cellular-automata program \cite[mitchell96] keeps an external genetic
algorithm as the selector and searches over physical laws; AlChemy
\cite[fontana94] derives replication from a chemistry but gives up space and
population structure. Each result is conditioned on what was
assumed.

We wanted the complementary experiment: fix one small physics whose
axioms contain {\it no evolutionary vocabulary at all}, put
everything else in matter, and determine --- by exhaustive search
where the worlds are small enough, and by controlled deletion
everywhere --- which evolutionary phenomena are genuinely properties
of law and which are properties of configuration. The claim we can
defend at the end is not “assumption-free” (nothing is) but
{\bf evolution-free assumptions}: five axioms about space, matter,
time, law, and drive, with selection, heredity, variation, and
parasitism appearing downstream as measured consequences.

\sec The physics in five axioms

The substrate is the execution model of the Zahradnice engine \cite[substrate26],
used unmodified throughout; the reader needs only its abstract
form:

\begitems
* \lawname{A1 Locality} Space is a row of cells on a ring (a
one-dimensional torus; we use rings of 6 to 96 cells). A rule reads
and writes a bounded neighbourhood and applies identically at every
position.
* \lawname{A2 Exclusion} Each cell holds exactly one symbol from a
finite alphabet, or is empty.
* \lawname{A3 Serialized choice} Time is a sequence of events. In
each event, all (rule, position) pairs whose pattern currently
matches are collected, and {\it exactly one} is applied, chosen at
random in proportion to rule weight. Because only one thing happens
per event, a “rate” can only ever be a {\it share} of the
currently applicable activity --- all processes pay from one budget.
(In the final worlds all weights are equal, so even the
weighted-choice generality goes unused.)
* \lawname{A4 Frozen law} The rule table is fixed before the world
starts and nothing in the world can change it. Whatever adapts,
adapts in configuration.
* \lawname{A5 Drive} The world is open: an external trigger erases
one uniformly random occupied cell --- a {\it wound} --- once every
$m{+}1$ events. We call $m$ the damage interval; its reciprocal
picture is the {\bf damage share} of the event budget: $m{=}8$
means wounds take about $11\%$ of all events, $m{=}4$ about
$20\%$, $m{=}2$ a third.
\enditems

One deliberate abstinence completes the setup: {\it the law has
no variables.} Every symbol a rule writes is a constant fixed when
the law was written; no rule can read a symbol from matter and
re-emit it. Writing is synthesis, never transport --- so copying,
if it is to exist, must be assembled in matter as an alphabet
case-split (one constant-writing rule per symbol, selected by
matching), carried through a material intermediary. Copying is
therefore derived, priced (a rule per symbol plus a carrier
state), and --- because it is assembled case by case --- mutable:
a one-symbol edit to one case yields systematic miscopying, which
is where night 10 will find its parasite.

Runs are seeded and bit-reproducible. Throughout, an
“experiment” is a sweep over damage shares, rule-set variants
(“arms”), and 16--64 random seeds; a run lasts 24{,}000--96{,}000
events unless stated.

\sec Two rules, read slowly

Most of the paper's vocabulary lives in one two-rule organism, so
we introduce it concretely. Take a ring, one wandering symbol \g{A}
(a {\it head}), and two rules:

\begitems
* {\bf Mover:} an \g{A} rewrites itself to \g{B} and writes a fresh
\g{A} into the cell to its east. Effect: the head steps east once
per firing, leaving a trail of \g{B} behind it --- and since the
ring wraps, it eventually laps its own trail and repaints it.
* {\bf Handler:} a \g{B} whose eastern neighbour is {\it empty}
rewrites itself to \g{A}. In an intact world this rule can never
fire --- the trail has no gaps --- so it is dormant matter. The
moment a wound (axiom A5) erases a cell, the trail cell west of the
hole satisfies the pattern and wakes: a new head is born at the
wound.
\enditems

That is the entire organism: mover $+$ handler, two rules. Poke it
anywhere and it heals; poke it repeatedly and --- as Section~6
measures --- it {\it multiplies}, because every wound can wake a
head and heads merge when they collide. When we later speak of a
{\it trail as a genome}, a {\it wound expressing stored variation},
or {\it repair as the only copying in the universe}, it is this
mechanical picture, elaborated: patterns that sit inert until
damage gives them a context, then act, then are overwritten by the
life that flows over them.

Numbers below come with natural units. Distances are in cells;
times in events, often restated as {\it laps} (events for a head to
circle its ring: 24 events for a one-step walker on our standard
24-cell ring) or as {\it wounds suffered}. “Final quarter” means
the last 25\% of a run's samples --- our standard steady-state
window.

\medskip
\noindent{\bf Notation.} {\it Event}: one applied rewrite.
{\it Wound}: one externally erased cell (axiom A5); damage share
$=1/(m{+}1)$. {\it Lap}: the events a head needs to circle its
ring. {\it Run}: a maximal block of equal symbols (an experimental
execution is also a “run”; context disambiguates). {\it Arm}: a
rule-set variant in a controlled comparison. {\it Dwell} (stroke
count) $k$: events the machinery spends per cell of a given type.
{\it Seed}: the RNG initialization; same-seed arms share initial
conditions.

\sec Method: search, exactness, controls

\secc Finding mechanisms instead of designing them

Early nights do not design rule sets; they enumerate them. A {\it
family} is a finite menu of rule shapes (rewrite in place; write
east; require east; combinations) closed under a compiler that can
only emit well-formed programs --- so every candidate parses and no
search time is spent on syntax. {\it Stratified exhaustion} then
runs every 1-rule set, every 2-rule set, and so on, stopping at the
first stratum containing a verified solution. The resulting claim,
“$k$ rules are the minimum {\it within family} $F$,” is
theorem-shaped with an explicitly declared scope --- stronger than
“smallest found”, weaker than “minimal absolutely”, and honest
about which.

\secc Exact accounting

Every run logs each applied rule. An independent replay ---
sharing nothing with the engine except the family definition ---
reconstructs the whole state trajectory from that log and compares
its final state byte-for-byte against the engine's own screen
dump. This check ran on every replayed run in the arc, about 26{,}000 of them, with zero mismatches. All measurements
(populations, order parameters, per-rule ledgers) are taken on the
replayed trajectory, so every number in this paper is backed by a
reconstruction that provably matches the machine.

\secc Controls, and the lessons that cost us wrong tables

Every “solution” found by a sweep is re-verified on held-out
seeds, longer horizons, and a second ring size before it is
believed. Two experiment designs recur: {\bf three-arm same-seed}
comparisons (identical initial conditions across rule-set variants,
paired statistics), and a {\bf three-probe} attribution pattern ---
a per-capita birth ledger, a per-capita death ledger, and
machinery-residence statistics conditioned on system state ---
which resolves “composition moved but every rate looks fair”
situations and twice overturned our own written predictions.

Two methodological results are reported because we paid for them.
{\it Power:} pilot effects at 16 seeds that looked systematic
evaporated at 64 seeds; every headline differential below uses 64
paired seeds. {\it Absorbing states:} observables sampled per event
are blind to extinction --- a dead run's samples simply stop, still
showing its last living population. Sixty extinctions were
misclassified as survivals until a conservation check (wounds
landed versus offered) exposed them. Standing rule: absorption is
judged from ground-truth final state, which the exactness check
pins. Two more traps earned entries during the theory work.
{\it Aliasing:} a probability current invisible at 50-event
sampling and unmistakable ($t\approx10$) at 5-event sampling ---
match the grid to the mechanism's timescale, and sample
deterministic cycles per event or at strides coprime to the
period. {\it Aggregation:} seed-averaged compositions that
satisfied an equilibrium law exactly turned out to be mixtures of
fixated monocultures, which satisfy it vacuously --- check
per-seed histograms for bimodality before calling anything an
equilibrium.

{\it Statistics, stated once.} Arm comparisons use the paired
same-seed design: per-seed differences, two-sided $t$, $n=64$
unless noted; “statistically zero” for the timing codon means all
six paired differentials had $|t|\le1.4$ ($p>0.16$). Proportions
are raw counts; intervals are 95\% normal approximations.
Exemplars: the parasite plateau $\rho^*=0.261\pm0.013$ (seed sd,
$n=32$, parasite-only, 20\% damage share; $0.204\pm0.014$ with
the honest codon); fixation of the slow gene at 64 seeds,
$0.47\pm0.12$ (dwell 2) and $0.55\pm0.12$ (dwell 3), both
against the neutral expectation 0.36 set by the initial share
(pooled $z\approx3.4$; the dwell ordering directional, not
separated at this power). Sweeps are exploratory and uncorrected
for multiplicity; the headline claims are saturated proportions
(240/240, 288/288, 24/24), robust to any correction. No mean-field
coefficient was fitted anywhere: the reduction's predictions are
parameter-free.

\sec Movement I: minimal mechanisms (nights 1--3)

\secc Night 1 --- calibration: the question defines the minimum

Night 1 asked something with a known answer --- the smallest
sustained oscillator --- over all 903 one- and two-rule programs of
a small family. The calibration paid by exposing an ambiguity in
the question itself. Under a {\it state-recurrence} predicate
(“the world revisits a configuration forever”) the minimum is one
rule: a walker, whose period is the ring circumference, courtesy of
the wrap. Under a {\it population-oscillation} predicate (“some
symbol count rises and falls forever”) the minimum is provably
two: the one-rule stratum is exhaustively empty. The predicate
defines the phenomenon; exhaustion prices it. The census also
delivered its first unplanned mechanism: a two-rule oscillator
whose first firing writes an inert partner cell and thereafter
{\it requires it as context} --- matter built once, then leaned on
as structure.

\secc Night 2 --- self-repair: one route, sixteen doors

Add wounds, and ask: what is the smallest rule set whose
established behaviour survives them? For static structures, one
rule suffices (a self-copier that saturates the ring and refills
holes). For {\it dynamic} behaviour --- something moving or
oscillating --- the answer is two rules, and the census of all
4{,}656 candidates found exactly 16 solutions, every one of them
anatomically the mover-plus-handler organism of Section~3: an
unconditional mover and a handler that can only fire beside a
wound. Damage-as-signal is not one design option among several;
within the family it is the {\it only} route to minimal dynamic
self-repair. Median healing time, measured on the 6-cell ring of
that census: five events --- under one lap. (Scaling splits by
anatomy, as night 3 shows: for the 14 mechanisms that respawn
adjacent to the wound, healing is a local race that does not know
the ring size; for the 2 that must walk to the wound, healing is
proportional to a lap.)

\secc Night 3 --- the phase diagram: collapse, and reproduction for free

Bombard the sixteen repairers with sustained damage. The survival
boundary sits between damage shares of one third and one half of
the event budget --- and because rates are shares (axiom A3), the
boundary is a {\it ratio}: it does not move with ring size. The
night-2 anatomy predicts robustness: adjacent-respawn mechanisms
are ring-independent; walk-to-wound mechanisms lose reliability on
the larger ring --- recovering only half the time, the
alive-but-altered outcome --- exactly as their healing-time
scaling implies (Figure~2).

The unplanned result sits mid-diagram: at intermediate damage
rates, the organism {\it multiplies} --- one walker becomes
several. Each wound can wake a handler into a new
head; heads merge on collision; the standing head count (about
three on a 12-cell ring at 25\% damage share) is set by wounding
rate against merge rate. No night had yet asked for reproduction.
Multiplication emerged as a corollary of repair in a damaging
world --- fragmentation reproduction, the planarian motif, in two rules.
(Strictly, proliferation --- multiplication without heredity;
heredity is night 4's addition.)

\sec Movement II: evolution assembled (nights 4--6)

\secc Night 4 --- heredity, dormancy, and the contest dichotomy

Put two copies of the organism on one ring with distinct symbol
pairs (heads \g{A}/\g{C}, trails \g{B}/\g{D}); movers overwrite
whatever they walk into, so the two lineages compete for territory.
The trail {\it is} the genome: wounds respawn heads from the local
trail, so lineages breed true by construction, and a lineage whose
heads all died but whose trail survives is not extinct --- it is
{\it dormant}, and a later wound resurrects it. At heavy damage
this seed-bank pathway carried the handicapped lineage through in
21 of 24 runs (median dormancy: 8.5 events).

Call an enabled rule instance {\bf contested} when some other
enabled instance overlaps it --- reads or writes one of the same
cells --- so that firing one changes or disables the other: which
fires first then decides a {\it state} outcome, and the weights,
which set firing order, become outcome probabilities. Call it
{\bf uncontested} when nothing enabled overlaps it: it commutes
with everything else happening, will fire sooner or later with
the same effect, and its weight shifts only timing. Every rule
competes for time (axiom A3); contested rules also compete for
state. This night's law is that only the second competition is
visible to selection (formal version: Section~9).

The law came from handicapping the same organism at two different
rules, with everything else identical. Halving or
quartering the {\it mover's} weight --- walk speed --- lost 240 of
240 runs at every damage share {\it including zero}: heads race
for the same cells at every single event, and the territory
boundary drifts at the weight difference, deciding the outcome
within the first lap or two (median extinction: 22 events).
Applying the same handicap to the {\it handler} --- repair speed
--- was invisible at every damage share: a wound waits for its
owner's handler, whose only competitor is a distant head walking
toward it, a race even a four-times-slower handler almost never
loses. \lawname{L2, first half} Selection acts on contested rates
only.

Night 9 completed the law's other cell with an arena of two
moverless tissues in which {\it both} neighbours of a wound can
claim it. Uncontested (west neighbour only), the handicap is
invisible in the strongest possible sense --- the runs are
bit-identical across handicap values, because every wound heals
before the next lands, so claim weights only permute healing
order. Contested, the same handicap is lethal: 24 of 24
extinctions. Same trait, same magnitudes; visibility decided
solely by whether two parties race for the same resource.
Population genetics states the coarse form of this law as {\it
soft selection} \cite[wallace75], and Eigen's constant-organization
constraint \cite[eigen71] is its quasispecies face; the substrate's
addition is exactness --- invisibility as bit-identity, not as weak
selection.

\secc Night 5 --- mutation: adaptation, its arrow, and the expression law

Add variation as law: rare trail-symbol flips, symmetric between
lineages, with the fitness difference on the contested rate (walk
speed). Start from the slow lineage alone; the fast one must {\it
arise} --- a mutant trail cell is invisible until a wound lands
beside it and the handler transcribes it into a living head.

Across 720 runs: with damage present, the fast lineage arose and
took sustained majority in {\bf 288 of 288} slow-start runs (the
first beneficial birth arriving after roughly a hundred wounds of
waiting at low mutation supply, down to a few dozen at high); under
the identical symmetric law, fast-start runs saw doomed slow
lineages born about 26 times per run and eaten every time --- {\bf
0 of 288} sustained reversals. And the control that became a law:
with no damage, {\bf 144 of 144} runs produce no birth ever, while
mutant genotype accumulates to 17\% of the trail and just sits
there. \lawname{L1} Stored variation is inert without damage:
wounding is simultaneously the selection pressure and the
transcription mechanism. (Biology knows this joint: bacterial
stress-induced mutagenesis couples mutation supply to damage
through the SOS response \cite[galhardo07].)

\secc Night 6 --- the stroke ladder: fitness without dials

Night 5's fitness was still a dial --- a weight attached to lineage
identity. Night 6 removed every dial. Three walking engines, all
weights equal, differing only in {\it anatomy}: one, two, or three
events per cell of progress (the extra events are wind-up strokes
that buy no distance). Under axiom A3 a three-stroke engine is a
third as fast {\it by structure}. \lawname{L3} Where all processes
pay from a shared event budget, events-per-outcome is a fitness ---
with no fitness function anywhere. (Digital evolution priced
fitness this way in practice --- Tierra's CPU-time economy
\cite[ray91], Avida's merit per gestation instruction
\cite[ofria04]; L3 is that economy stated as law, with no organism
boundary, no scheduler, and all weights equal.)

Started from the slowest engine with symmetric mutation among the
three trail symbols, the ladder was climbed in {\bf 72 of 72}
moderate-damage runs (population mean speed rising threefold to the
optimum), and mostly by {\it leapfrog}: direct worst-to-best
transitions outnumbered sequential ascent two to one, since a
direct mutation path exists and a newborn one-stroke walker outruns
a two-stroke sweep already in progress --- clonal interference,
visible in the lineage traces. Heavy damage turned the same setup
into {\bf evolutionary rescue} \cite[bell09,gonzalez13]: the slow resident is
subcritical there (night 3's collapse threshold, reached through
anatomy), dying at a median of 237 events --- about three of its
own laps --- unless a faster engine arises {\it and establishes}
first. Rescue probability was non-monotone in mutation supply
(5, then 8, then 3 of 24 as supply rose) --- the
rescue-to-lethal-mutagenesis crossover that population-genetic
theory derives from an optimal mutation rate \cite[anciaux19],
reproduced here in a substrate with no fitness function --- because
at the top of that range even the {\it optimal} engine, installed
as resident, died in 19 of 24 runs: an {\bf error catastrophe}
\cite[eigen71] in the demographic, lethal-mutagenesis sense
\cite[bull07] rather than the informational one, with a distinct
signature: slow, load-driven decay over hundreds of laps,
against the sharp three-lap structural collapse. Mutation is
rescuer and executioner at once; even failed rescues stretched
survival by an order of magnitude.

\sec Movement III: the author dissolved (nights 7--8)

\secc Night 7 --- the tape commons: neutral flows, and residence

Now remove lineages, mutation, and every remaining asymmetry. The
ring is a circular chromosome of genes \g{f} and \g{s}; a fixed
population of {\it polymerase} heads patrols it eastward, crossing
\g{f} in one event and \g{s} in two (a settle stroke --- anatomy
again, not weight); wounds strike the tape only; and {\it the only
copying in the universe is repair}: a head standing beside a hole
writes a copy of the gene it covers into it. Nine uniform rules.
Variation is the random initial tape. The unauthored game: \g{f}
is a public good (fast tape speeds every head); \g{s} taxes the
commons.

Our design notes predicted a tragedy of the commons via
replication bursts. The three-probe attribution refuted it:
per-capita replication is {\it neutral} (0.98--1.00 against
instantaneous composition) and so is per-capita death
(0.99--1.00). Yet composition moves --- so the motion must live in
state structure, and it does: at a one-third damage share the
system spends about 90\% of its time nearly wiped, and in that
regime the machinery is found covering \g{s} 82\% of the time
(covered genes cannot be wounded, and settling on \g{s} doubles
the dwell), while the rare regrowth episodes are almost pure
\g{f}-work. Heavy damage does not select slowness within a living
commons; it destroys the commons, and slowness survives as what
the machinery happens to be sheltering.

Night 9 probed the scaling: a {\it three}-stroke \g{s} lifts
machinery residence further, and the outcomes shift with it.
Understanding {\it what} shifts took one more measurement ---
charting the residence map $R(x)$ along relaxation trajectories
(Section~9, Figure~4) shows $R$ strictly above composition
throughout the interior, so these worlds do not equilibrate at
all: they {\it fixate}, and the honest statistics are fixation
counts --- at 64 seeds the slow gene fixes in $0.47\pm0.12$ of
dwell-2 worlds and $0.55\pm0.12$ of dwell-3 worlds, both above
the neutral expectation 0.36 set by the initial composition
(pooled $z\approx3.4$). \lawname{L4} The machinery's residence
excess over composition is a selection differential:
slow-to-process genes are favoured in proportion to structural
dwell, up to fixation --- a tragedy of the commons \cite[hardin68] whose tax
collector is time itself. The queueing half of the mechanism is
old --- slow sites jam traffic in the ribosome-flow models that
founded exclusion-process physics \cite[macdonald68] --- and
slowness-as-shelter from an activity-seeking hazard is bacterial
persistence \cite[balaban04]; what the substrate composes is the
full chain, dwell to shelter to fixation of {\it information},
with its sign condition explicit: under a pure copy-rate clock the
fast-processed wins instead, as in Spiegelman's serial-transfer
experiment \cite[mills67]. (Two candidate equilibrium readings
dissolved on the way, one into relaxation under a longer horizon,
one into the fixation mixture just described; both dissolutions
are reported because the arc's credibility lives in them.)

\secc Night 8 --- closing the loop: what heredity is made of

Can a sequence program the machinery that copies it? A three-arm
same-seed experiment against a measured null. The null is itself a
discovery: with no codon at all, damage plus copy-local repair
{\it homogenizes} the genome (mean run of identical symbols
growing from 2.2 to 8.9 cells on a 24-ring --- near-total
coarsening), because every repair copies a run's edge outward. Any
codon claim must beat that ratchet.

Two codons were added, separately, both uniform and
symbol-symmetric; each is two rules, and the nine-rule base plus
one codon is the eleven-rule world of the subtitle. The {\bf timing codon} gives a head that is
reading a run (same symbol behind it) a second, co-applicable
repair rule --- doubling repair's share of the event budget at run
sites, {\it without changing what is written}. It carried 40--43\%
of all repairs and did nothing: every paired differential is
statistically zero at 64 seeds. The reason is visible in the rule
text --- both rules write the same symbol, so the codon modulates
only {\it when} a hole closes, and fill identity is
timing-independent. The {\bf content codon} lets a head reading a
run repair a two-cell gap with {\it two copies in one event} ---
changing what is written. It fired in only 9--21\% of repairs and
moved every observable first-order: commons health up 28\% at a
20\% damage share, up 60\% in the collapse regime (paired
$t\approx4$, $n{=}64$ throughout), run structure above the null at
every damage share, and --- night 9's fine grid --- one full step
of viability at the collapse boundary. \lawname{L5} Heritable
information --- in every codon tested --- is exactly the part of
pattern whose reading changes what the machinery writes. Reading
frequency is irrelevant; consequence of reading is everything. L2
and L5 are one law seen from two sides: an uncontested rate {\it
is} a schedule. (L5 also answers a standing objection: compositional
ensembles without template copying are argued to lack evolvability
\cite[vasas10]; the criterion says which non-template patterns are
heritable anyway --- echoing von Neumann's dual use of the
description, copied uninterpreted, read interpreted
\cite[vonneumann66].)

\sec Movement IV: ecology without an author (night 10)

Deception entered as two one-character edits of night 8's honest
codons --- in this substrate the distance between fidelity and
parasitism is literally one symbol.

The {\bf mutator} (the timing codon with its context flipped: at a
run's {\it edge}, the western gene reaches through the machinery
and claims the hole, making the fill a 50/50 contest) fails by
starvation. It feeds on boundaries between runs, and boundaries
are what the coarsening ratchet destroys: all mutator arms
crystallize to a single-symbol world, the mutator ending with
under 2\% of repairs. A deception that consumes its own context is
self-limiting.

The {\bf parasite} (the content codon with its {\it output}
flipped: the same run-reading context, but the double yield spent
on {\it wrong} copies) is fueled by order itself, and it cannot
starve. Its arms never crystallize. The boundary density $\rho$
--- boundaries per adjacent gene pair, so $0$ is a one-species
crystal and $0.5$ is coin-flip disorder --- stabilizes --- at $\rho^*\approx0.26$ parasite-only,
$\approx0.20$ with the honest codon competing --- at {\it every}
damage share (Figure~3), against $\rho=0$ in
every parasite-free arm, and fluctuates forever around that level
(the fluctuation amplitude grows with damage share while the level
does not --- Section~9 explains both). The cycle is visible in the
trajectories: coarsening grows runs; runs regenerate the
parasite's reading context; wrong copies re-seed boundaries; local
order collapses; coarsening resumes. And the cycle is not a
manner of speaking: sampled at the mechanism's own timescale the
trajectory violates detailed balance outright ($t\approx-10$ at
five-event lags; Section~9) --- a circulating probability current
that no reversible rule set could produce. The parasite is
simultaneously a {\it matter benefactor} and an {\it information
corruptor}: its commons health matches the honest-codon arms and
beats the null in the collapse regime, because wrong copies still
fill wounds. Two corollaries. First, coexistence. Night 4's impossibility is
the small theorem it sounds like: under overwrite competition the
monocultures are absorbing and reachable, so fixation is almost
sure --- gambler's ruin. Here the ordered state is {\it not}
absorbing (a whole-ring run is itself parasite context: the
crystal leaks), and with at least two heads the reachable set
contains no absorbing state at all; “permanent” means exactly
that --- recurrence of the mixed phase, structural in the law and
observed in 128 of 128 parasite-arm runs, in the family of
diversity-under-disturbance results ecology knows \cite[connell78].
Replicator chemistry knows one non-spatial route to permanent
coexistence --- sub-exponential ({\it parabolic}) growth, whose
per-capita advantage of rarity yields survival of everybody
\cite[szathmary89,paczko24] --- and this phase is measurably not
that: parabolic kinetics is still autocatalytic, so extinction
stays absorbing, while here the minority gene is reborn {\it while
extinct} --- every zero-abundance birth arrives through the
deceptive pair, and the honest-codon control shows none in two
million extinct-state events (audit:
\repo{experiments/inverse/night10-exponent-results.md}).
Coexistence is influx-stabilized, and the influx is conditioned on
the order it corrupts. And the parasite arms are the only worlds of the arc that
never exhaust their variation: standing diversity is generated and
regulated by the system itself, with no mutation operator
anywhere. \lawname{L6} Deception persists iff it is fueled by the
order it attacks --- parasitology's oldest rule, and signaling
theory's \cite[searcy05]: deception lives off the honesty it
exploits, at a level the honesty can afford. Here it is derived in
thirteen rules.

\secc Contest is a statement about critical pairs

In rewriting vocabulary, an overlap of enabled instances is a
{\it critical pair}. Proposition (schedule invisibility): if,
between consecutive external events, the enabled instances are
pairwise non-overlapping and each phase runs to completion, then
the inter-event state is independent of the rule weights ---
non-overlapping applications commute, so by Newman's lemma the
normal form is unique and weights only permute the path to it; no
outcome functional can depend on them. Selection therefore
requires critical pairs. Night 9's uncontested arms ---
bit-identical across a four-fold handicap --- are the hypothesis
side of this proposition holding exactly; its contested arms'
24/24 are the converse. Nights 4 and 8 are the same statement at
two scales: a persistently contested mover against a
critical-pair-free handler; a content codon that changes what is
written against a timing codon that merely reorders it.

\secc A mean-field reduction of the parasite phase (L6)

Let $\rho$ be boundary density and $\delta$ the damage share. Three
processes move $\rho$. {\it Coarsening:} a wound plus its repair
replaces a random cell by a copy of its western neighbour; in a
mean-field of independent adjacent pairs this changes the expected
boundary count by $-2\rho^2$ per repair --- a quadratic loss,
which reproduces the null's behaviour: crystallization with a slow
tail, $\rho=0$ absorbing. {\it Gap bursts} add a loss linear in
$\rho$ (a multi-cell gap refills from one flank, deleting any
boundary it spanned). {\it Deception:} an infidelity event creates
about two boundaries, at a rate proportional to its {\it fuel}
term --- $\rho$ for the mutator (it needs a boundary to read),
$(1-\rho)$ for the parasite (it needs a run). Hence

$$
\dot\rho \;=\; 2\,c_d\,\delta\,F(\rho)\;-\;2\,c_0\,\delta\,\rho^2
\;-\;c_1\,\delta\,\rho, \qquad
F(\rho)=\cases{\rho & mutator \cr 1-\rho & parasite.}
$$

Two structural consequences follow immediately, and both were
measured before this reduction was written. First, the {\bf
dichotomy}: for the parasite, $F(0)=1>0$, so the flow at the
crystal is strictly positive --- $\rho=0$ is not absorbing and an
interior equilibrium $\rho^*$ exists for any nonzero deception
coefficient. For the mutator, $F(0)=0$: gain and the linear loss
compete at the same order, and the crystal remains absorbing
whenever bursts and coarsening dominate --- which the data decided
(the mutator's measured share never reached 2\%). The sign of the
fuel term at the ordered state is the whole law. Second, the {\bf
two robustnesses}: every term carries the same factor $\delta$, so
the damage share sets the {\it clock} but cancels from the
equilibrium condition --- predicting a plateau level insensitive
to damage share and ring size, with fluctuation {\it amplitude}
growing with $\delta$ (fewer, larger stochastic kicks per unit of
relaxation). Both match: $\rho^*$ is arm-dependent but constant across
damage shares and both rings ($0.26$ parasite-only, $0.20$ with
the honest codon); second-half standard deviation
rising from 0.08 to 0.20 as damage share triples. (The reduction
assumes independent adjacent pairs while Section~9.4 measures
correlations beyond any order-1 model; there is no contradiction
--- the consequences used are structural, the sign of $F(0)$ and
the $\delta$-cancellation, not quantitative --- but the mean-field
succeeds despite, not because of, its independence assumption.)

The current implied by the interior state is measured directly:
time-reversal asymmetry of the (boundary density, tape level)
trajectory is null at the paper's standard 50-event sampling and
decisive at 5-event sampling ($t=-10.5$ at lag one in the
two-codon world, decaying within $\approx$40 events --- the
current lives at the timescale of individual repair episodes,
which coarser grids alias away). The parasite-only world adds
structure: the asymmetry {\it reverses sign} with lag ($-9.3$ at
lag one, $+5.2$ at lag sixteen) --- two nested, counter-rotating
circulation loops, a fast wound-repair cycle inside a slow
build-and-corrupt cycle, the slow loop suppressed when the honest
codon is present. Detailed balance is decisively violated; a
reversible grammar cannot have this phase.

\secc The stationary word is not order-1 Markov

Two tests against the maximum-entropy null at the measured
boundary density. Run lengths: singletons suppressed and pairs
enriched relative to the geometric distribution in all four
measured cells --- the pair-codon writes wrong copies in pairs,
and the word keeps that signature. Correlations: $C(1)$ matches
the Markov value by construction, then the measured $C(d)$
collapses faster than $(1-2\rho)^d$ and turns negative at
$d=3$--$5$ (deepest $-0.16$). A two-state chain can alternate
sign, but only with $C(1)<0$; the measured $C(1)>0$ forces any
order-1 fit to $C(d)=C(1)^d>0$ at every lag, so the negative lobe
excludes order-1 outright. The stationary
language of the parasite phase has liquid-like short-range order
at a characteristic domain scale of two to three cells: the phenomenon climbs the weighted-language hierarchy, and the
climb is mechanistic --- the word statistics carry the generating
codon's signature.

\secc The residence law: a derivation, a self-refutation, and a better law

Write the composition balance. Deaths strike visible genes
composition-uniformly. Births write the gene the machinery covers;
split them by the covered value's provenance --- freshly inherited
at the wound's flank (composition-distributed) versus carried
through the head's dwell (residence-distributed, share
$\lambda>0$). Then
$$
\dot x \;\propto\; \lambda\,\bigl(R(x)-x\bigr),
$$
and the channel weights, previously flagged as the open unknown,
cancel: any equilibrium must satisfy $x^*=R(x^*)$, and away from
one the residence excess $R(x)-x$ is a selection differential ---
a Fisher-like drift with no fitness function anywhere.

The empirical part then corrected us twice, and the corrections
improved the law. On a frozen tape the residence map is pure
traversal dwell, $R(x)=kx/(1-x+kx)$ --- verified exactly. On the
live tape, charting $(x,R)$ windows along relaxation trajectories
yields a map (Figure~4) lying strictly {\it between} identity
and the dwell curve ($R-x\approx+0.10$ to $+0.22$ across the interior, larger
at dwell 3 than dwell 2), so at these damage shares $R$ has {\it
no interior fixed point}: both monocultures are absorbing and the
worlds fixate. That exposed our own earlier check as vacuous ---
the seed-averaged cells in which composition appeared to track
residence were mixtures of fixated monocultures, which satisfy
$R=x$ identically (the aggregation trap of Section~4). What
survives is stronger than what fell: the drift law, with the
charted excess as its selection coefficient, is borne out at 64 seeds: the slow gene
fixes above the neutral expectation set by the initial composition
($0.47\pm0.12$ at dwell 2, $0.55\pm0.12$ at dwell 3, against
neutral 0.36; pooled $z\approx3.4$), the dwell ordering
directional but not separated at this power. The one open
calculation left is the live map itself: a renewal analysis of the
head's action cycle with self-consistent hole density, its target
curve now measured.

\sec The six laws, substrate-free

\noindent Several of these laws have kin in older literatures ---
soft selection for L2, digital evolution's fitness economies for
L3, queueing and persistence for L4, the interpreted/uninterpreted
description for L5, frequency-dependent deception for L6; the
kinships are cited where each law is earned, and Section~13 draws
the wider map. The statements below are the exact,
substrate-derived forms.

\begitems
* \lawname{L1 Expression} In machinery-mediated copying systems,
stored variation enters phenotype space only through repair
events: damage is simultaneously selection pressure and
transcription. No damage, no evolution --- however much variation
the genome carries.
* \lawname{L2 Contest} A rate parameter is under selection iff two
processes race for the same resource under one clock. An
uncontested rate is a schedule; schedules are invisible to
selection however large their differences.
* \lawname{L3 Structural fitness} Where all processes pay from a
shared event budget, the number of events a mechanism spends per
unit of outcome is a fitness, with no fitness function anywhere:
anatomy is selectable with all rates equal.
* \lawname{L4 Residence} With protection or expression coupled
to the copying machinery's position, the machinery's residence
excess over composition is a selection differential:
slow-to-process information is favoured in proportion to
structural dwell, up to fixation, even in a healthy system ---
with no fitness function anywhere.
* \lawname{L5 Heredity} Heritable information is exactly the
part of pattern whose reading changes what the machinery writes;
reading frequency is irrelevant. (We advance L5 as a definition
the measurements reward --- demonstrated for both codons tested
--- rather than as a proven biconditional.)
* \lawname{L6 Deception} Infidelity persists iff its expression is
fueled by the order it corrupts. Disorder-fueled deception starves
on its own success; order-fueled deception self-regulates into
permanent coexistence with its host --- and thereby into a
self-sustaining variation supply.
\enditems

\sec Honest limits

\begitems
* {\bf The codon tables are still law-side.} Nights 8 and 10 made
codon {\it expression} pattern-dependent, but the map from context
to consequence is authored and uniform. A codon table in matter
--- semantics the tape itself can rewrite --- is the identified
next remove, and likely spends the substrate's so-far-unused
second dimension.
* {\bf One dimension, two-symbol genomes, rings of 6--96 cells.}
The parasite phase is intensive in ring size at equal machinery
density, with a measured machinery floor at the collapse edge;
other results state their ring scope explicitly.
Collapse-regime composition depends on the absolute machinery
count (the remnant is what the machines cover), and is stated per
configuration.
* {\bf Theory status is uneven by design.} L6 has a reduction
that explains the measured robustnesses and a directly measured
current; L2 has a proof sketch via critical pairs; L4 is a
derived drift law whose coefficient --- the live residence map ---
is charted but not yet derived (a renewal calculation with a
measured target); L3 carries a registered falsification condition
(synchronous updating). The law-side ablation program and the
hierarchy formalization are deferred to a successor paper by
scope, not difficulty.
* {\bf Negative results are load-bearing.} The timing codon's
null, the mutator's starvation, the handler's invisibility, and
two dissolved selection signals are not caveats; they are how the
laws were isolated. Where our written predictions failed (night
7's tragedy mechanism, night 3's repair-selection forecast), the
failure is reported beside its resolution.
* {\bf Instrument scope.} The engine loses three to five orders of
magnitude of runtime to specialized kernels \cite[substrate26]; what that buys
here is exhaustive small-world search with exact accounting for
free. Exponent-grade statistical physics of these models belongs
to dedicated code, validated against our replay.
\enditems

\sec Related work

AlChemy \cite[fontana94] derived replicators from a chemistry without space;
Tierra \cite[ray91] and Avida \cite[ofria04] evolve authored replicators; the EvCA
program \cite[mitchell96] searches over CA rule tables with an external genetic
algorithm. The present arc differs from each on one axis:
replication is never a primitive (it emerges as repair, night 3,
and becomes programmable, night 8); the law is never searched or
mutated (all search and all evolution happen in matter under one
fixed physics); and there is no external selection loop ---
selection, where it occurs, is paid from the same event budget as
everything else, in the spirit of asynchronous-CA studies \cite[fates14,blok99,nehaniv04,fates05]
with the event measure promoted from nuisance to subject. The
promotion has a formal pedigree: A3's event semantics --- one
weighted choice among all enabled instances --- is verbatim the
race policy of generalized stochastic Petri nets \cite[marsan84],
and its continuous-time parents are the Gillespie jump chain
\cite[gillespie77] and the Moran process \cite[moran58]; ecology
has even used the strict shared budget to {\it null} selection
(zero-sum neutral drift \cite[hubbell01]). None of these treats
the event measure as the contested resource itself; that
serialization alone can decide evolutionary outcomes has been
plain since asynchronous updating erased spatial cooperation
\cite[huberman93]. A
formal kinship deserves its own sentence: in one dimension,
spatial adjacency {\it is} string adjacency, so these worlds are
weighted stochastic string-rewriting (semi-Thue) systems on
circular words \cite[newman42,book93] --- sequential rewriting under a weighted
strategy, walls as end markers, the whole arc a study of
stationary processes of a small context-sensitive fragment. The
mapping breaks precisely at two dimensions, where graph rewriting
begins, and the critical-pair reading of L2 (Section~9) is its
first dividend. The nearest neighbour of the coexistence result is
the spatial host--parasite literature of RNA-world models, where
parasites are contained by emergent pattern formation --- spiral
waves \cite[boerlijst91], travelling waves and multilevel structure \cite[takeuchi12] --- in
two dimensions, where parasites end up sustaining and even
complexifying their hosts \cite[colizzi16,hickinbotham21] --- the
same conclusion as L6's with the causal arrow reversed: there,
parasite-made emptiness fuels host adaptation; here, host-made
order fuels the parasite. Our claim is deliberately sharper, coexistence on
a bare one-dimensional ring with no pattern-forming mechanism
available, carried by the fuel structure of the deception alone
(L6). The claim's classical foil is Maynard Smith's: in a
well-mixed system the undiscriminating parasite simply wins
\cite[ms79]; the known escapes are two-dimensional pattern,
compartments, and parabolic kinetics \cite[szathmary89], the last
excluded by measurement in Section~8. The nearest existing
coexistence without compartments is Tierra's \cite[ray91], whose
parasites are also order-fueled --- they execute their hosts' copy
loops --- and oscillate rather than fixate; but that coexistence
rides an authored copy loop, per-organism CPU slices, an external
mutation operator and lineage-aware reaping, all absent here, and
no analogue of the damage-share-invariant plateau is reported.
Endogenous variation supplied by machinery that miswrites has an
ALife ancestor in the machine--tape networks of Ikegami and
Hashimoto \cite[ikegami95]; L6 adds the persistence criterion ---
which miswriters survive their own success. Artificial chemistries
broadly are reviewed in \cite[dittrich01]; the nearest rewriting
chemistry with a damage drive is Squirm3, whose evolution is
sustained by periodic floods \cite[hutton02]; and the census
method has a direct precedent in exhaustive small-rule-space
enumeration against formal behavioural predicates \cite[adams17]
--- the discipline the replicators-from-random-soups line
\cite[aguera24] is currently relearning. Beside the night-2 census
sits the self-repairing-loop lineage of Langton and Sayama
\cite[langton84,sayama99], with the difference that our repairers
are found by exhaustion, not construction; and night 3's
fragmentation reproduction has cousins wherever driven structures
divide without templates --- Gray--Scott spot division
\cite[pearson93], breakage-propagated filaments \cite[knowles09],
and the damage-trained planarian regeneration of neural cellular
automata \cite[mordvintsev20], the last engineered by gradient
descent where ours falls out of two censused rules. The individual phenomena
connect to their own literatures: error
catastrophe and parasite-threatened replicators to Eigen and the
hypercycle \cite[eigen71,eigen79] --- whose classical rescue, compartments, is
exactly what our measured parasite now motivates; evolutionary
rescue to Bell and Gonzalez \cite[bell09,gonzalez13] and its
non-monotone mutation dependence to Anciaux et al.
\cite[anciaux19]; clonal interference to Gerrish
and Lenski \cite[gerrish98]; the commons to Hardin \cite[hardin68]; coarsening and
majority amplification to voter models \cite[clifford73]; damage-coupled
mutation supply to stress-induced mutagenesis \cite[galhardo07];
diversity-under-disturbance to Connell \cite[connell78]; gene-level
bookkeeping to Dawkins \cite[dawkins76]; the ladder of removes to the
major-transitions program \cite[msz95]; dormancy to seed-bank ecology
\cite[cohen66]; heredity-as-consequence (L5) to semantic
information under counterfactual scrambling \cite[kolchinsky18]
and to the derived central-dogma asymmetry \cite[takeuchi19]; the
measured circulating current to landscape--flux analyses of
eco-evolutionary models \cite[xu21] and to cycling measured in
game experiments \cite[wang14] --- equation- and agent-level
precedents; we know of no prior configuration-space current
measured inside an artificial-life substrate; and the summit the
arc walks toward --- self-copying
structure without a copy primitive --- to von Neumann \cite[vonneumann66].

\sec Where this leaves the project

The arc set out to measure what evolution needs from law. The
running answer: locality, exclusion, serialized choice, a frozen
rule table, and a drive --- five axioms with no evolutionary
vocabulary --- plus configurations. Selection, heredity, dormancy,
adaptation, rescue, catastrophe, tragedy, and parasitism are then
properties of matter, several provably minimal within their
declared families. Four continuations are queued: the axiom-ablation matrix
(subtract law-side axioms as this paper subtracted matter-side
ingredients --- each law should survive exactly the ablations of
the axioms it does not consume); the formal program (the
semi-Thue treatment, the critical-pair theorem, the residence
renewal calculation --- rigor deferred here by scope, not
difficulty); the codon table in matter; and compartments ---
biology's answer to parasites \cite[eigen79,msz95] --- built as walls of
matter, in a world that finally contains a parasite to test them
against.

\sec Appendix: the substrate and the families, formally

{\bf State.} A configuration is a map $c:{\bf Z}_N\to\Sigma\cup\{\sqr\}$
on a ring of $N$ cells; $\sqr$ is the empty cell, $\Sigma$ the
world's alphabet (census and lineage worlds: matter symbols such as
\g{A},\g{B},\dots; tape world: genes \g{f},\g{s} and machinery
states \g{F},\g{S},\g{W},\g{V}).

{\bf Rules.} A rule is $r=(a,\tau,P_r,O_r,w)$: an anchor symbol
$a\in\Sigma$, a trigger class $\tau$ (working event or wound), a
read set $P_r$ of (offset, required symbol) pairs, a write set
$O_r$ of (offset, written symbol) pairs, and a weight $w$ (equal to
1 for every rule of the final worlds). $r$ is {\it enabled} at
position $i$ iff $c(i)=a$ and $c(i{+}d)=g$ for every $(d,g)\in P_r$.
Applying it sets $c(i{+}d):=g'$ for every $(d,g')\in O_r$. All
offsets are in $\{-1,0,+1,+2\}$; there are no variables (Section 2):
every written symbol is a constant of the rule.

{\bf Events.} A working event collects all enabled
(rule, position) pairs with $\tau=$ work and applies exactly one,
sampled with probability proportional to $w$. A wound event does the same over the
erase family $\{(g,\hbox{wound},\emptyset,\{(0,\sqr)\},1):
g\in\Sigma_w\}$ for a declared $\Sigma_w\subseteq\Sigma$ ---
one uniformly random erasable cell becomes empty. Census and
lineage worlds take $\Sigma_w=\Sigma$; the tape world takes
$\Sigma_w=\{f,s\}$, leaving machinery wound-immune. The drive interleaves one wound per $m{+}1$
events.

{\bf Shapes.} Every rule of the paper is an instance of seven
shapes ($\varrho$ = the anchor's replacement symbol; $C,W,X$
symbols or $\sqr$):

\medskip
\noindent\table{lll}{
shape & reads & writes \crl\noalign{\smallskip}
self & --- & $0{\mapsto}\varrho$ \cr
write($X$) & --- & $0{\mapsto}\varrho,\ {+}1{\mapsto}X$ \cr
req($C$) & ${+}1{\mapsto}C$ & $0{\mapsto}\varrho$ \cr
reqwrite($C,X$) & ${+}1{\mapsto}C$ & $0{\mapsto}\varrho,\ {+}1{\mapsto}X$ \cr
wreqwrite($W,C,X$) & ${-}1{\mapsto}W,\ {+}1{\mapsto}C$ & $0{\mapsto}\varrho,\ {+}1{\mapsto}X$ \cr
gapwrite($W,X$) & ${-}1{\mapsto}W,\ {+}1{\mapsto}\sqr,\ {+}2{\mapsto}\sqr$ & $0{\mapsto}\varrho,\ {+}1{\mapsto}X,\ {+}2{\mapsto}X$ \cr
westclaim($X$) & ${-}1{\mapsto}\sqr$ & $0{\mapsto}\varrho,\ {-}1{\mapsto}X$ \cr
}
\medskip

{\bf Families.} With $\Sigma=\{A,B\}$ and
$\varrho\in\{A,B,\sqr\}$: $F_1$ is the set of all rules of
shapes self, write($X$), req($C$) with $X,C\in\{A,B,\sqr\}$ ---
$2\times3\times(1{+}3{+}3)=42$ rules; $F_2=F_1\cup{}$reqwrite
($2\times3\times9=54$ more, 96 total). A genotype is a subset of
the family; stratum $k$ is all $k$-subsets; minimality claims
quantify over exactly these sets, with the wound family as fixed
harness in both.

{\bf The tape world} (nights 7--10), complete law. The
“nine uniform rules” of Section 7.1 count the wound pair, damage
being constitutive there (the convention of the Section 10 ledger
note); numbered:

\begitems\style n
* $F$-mover onto $f$: $(F,\hbox{reqwrite}(f,F),\varrho{=}f)$
* $F$-mover onto $s$: $(F,\hbox{reqwrite}(s,W),\varrho{=}f)$
* $S$-mover onto $f$: $(S,\hbox{reqwrite}(f,F),\varrho{=}s)$
* $S$-mover onto $s$: $(S,\hbox{reqwrite}(s,W),\varrho{=}s)$
* settle: $(W,\hbox{self},\varrho{=}S)$; the dwell-3 variant
inserts $W{\to}V{\to}S$ (ten rules there)
* repair over $f$: $(F,\hbox{reqwrite}(\sqr,f),\varrho{=}F)$
* repair over $s$: $(S,\hbox{reqwrite}(\sqr,s),\varrho{=}S)$
* wound on $f$: $(f,\hbox{wound},\varrho{=}\sqr)$
* wound on $s$: $(s,\hbox{wound},\varrho{=}\sqr)$
\enditems

\noindent Each codon of Sections 7--8 adds its symbol-symmetric
wreqwrite or gapwrite pair (9${+}$2 $=$ the subtitle's eleven;
9${+}$4 $=$ thirteen with both). Machinery states appear in no
erase rule and no rule removes them: heads are eternal, which is
where the machinery floor of the parasite phase (Section~8)
enters.
