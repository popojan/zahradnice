\tit The Subtraction Ladder

\centerline{\it Ten nights of inverse emergence: repair, heredity,
and parasitism in eleven rules}
\medskip
\centerline{Jan Popelka \& Claude (Anthropic) --- August 2026}
\centerline{\tt github.com/popojan/zahradnice \quad branch {\tt research}
\quad \repo{experiments/inverse/}}
\bigskip

\noindent{\bf Abstract.}
How much of evolution must be written into physical law, and how
much is a property of matter? We attack the question by deletion. The
instrument is a deliberately small physics: an asynchronous
stochastic rewriting system on a one-dimensional toroidal ring in
which every process --- movement, repair, copying, damage --- pays
from a single shared event budget. Over ten experiments (“nights”)
we first {\it find} minimal mechanisms by exhaustive search over
admissible rule families, then compose them into evolving systems,
then delete the authored ingredients one by one: mechanism
libraries, rate dials, lineage identities, mutation operators,
fitness functions, copy primitives. The end state is a family of
worlds of nine to thirteen uniform, glyph-symmetric rules that
exhibit natural selection, heredity with a dormant seed bank,
adaptation with a measurable arrow, evolutionary rescue, error
catastrophe, a tragedy of the commons with a structural threshold,
and finally parasitism that enforces permanent coexistence and a
self-sustaining variation supply --- none of it named in the law.
Six laws survive translation out of the substrate and are the
paper's contributions, among them: stored variation is inert without
damage (wounding is the expression mechanism); selection acts on
contested rates only (an uncontested rate is a schedule, and
schedule is invisible); heritable information is exactly the part
of pattern whose reading changes what the machinery writes; and
deception persists iff it is fueled by the order it attacks. Every
quantitative claim is backed by exact event-level accounting:
independently replaying each run's trace reconstructs the final
state byte-for-byte (about 25{,}000 runs, zero mismatches), and the
whole arc cost minutes of laptop compute per experiment.

\sec The question, and the instrument

Models of evolution usually begin by assuming its ingredients:
replicators exist, fitness is a function, mutation is an operator,
selection is imposed by the experimenter's loop. Artificial-life
systems relax some assumptions and keep others --- Tierra and Avida
[2,3] author the replicator and let selection emerge; evolving
cellular automata [4] author the selection loop and search over
laws; AlChemy [1] derives replication from a chemistry but gives up
space and population structure. We wanted the complementary
experiment: fix one small, uniform physics once, put {\it
everything else in matter}, and measure --- by exhaustive search
where possible, by controlled deletion everywhere --- which
evolutionary phenomena are genuinely law-side and which are
configuration.

The physics is the execution model of the Zahradnice engine [20],
used unmodified throughout: a screen of characters is the entire
state; a program is a fixed set of local rewriting rules; execution
is a jump chain in which each event gathers every (rule, position)
pair whose pattern matches and applies exactly one, sampled in
proportion to rule weight. Two properties matter for everything
below. First, the {\bf shared event measure}: there is no other
clock, so every process pays for its activity from the same budget,
and “rate” is always a share, never an absolute. Second, the {\bf
laws/matter boundary}: rules are fixed at load time; nothing a
program does can change them. Adaptation, if it happens, happens in
configuration.

All ten experiments live on a one-dimensional ring (a single row of
the toroidal screen; rules read and write east/west neighbours).
Damage is part of the law: a dedicated trigger erases one
weight-uniform random cell of matter per poke, at an interval $m$
(one poke per $m{+}1$ events) that serves as the environmental
harshness dial. We stayed in 1-D deliberately --- the second screen
dimension remains in reserve --- and it never ran out: the last
nights' results (a parasite phase with a stochastic limit cycle)
happen on the same single row as the first night's two-rule
oscillators.

A note on claims. Results are stated for this class of system ---
asynchronous stochastic rewriting under a shared event measure with
local matter and immutable law --- and each of the six laws is
restated substrate-free in Section~8. Zahradnice is the instrument,
not the subject.

\sec Method: inverse emergence with exact accounting

\secc Families, and what “minimal” may honestly claim

Instead of writing programs, we write {\it family compilers}: a
genotype is a set of rules drawn from a finite menu of shapes
(rewrite in place; write east; require east; require-and-write;
two-cell context forms added as later nights needed them), and a
compiler emits the runnable program. Everything the compiler can
emit parses and has well-formed geometry --- admissibility by
construction, no mutate-then-repair. Minimality claims come from
{\bf stratified exhaustion}: run every $k$-rule genotype for
$k=1,2,\ldots$ and stop at the first stratum with a verified
satisfier. This proves “minimum within family $F$” --- a
theorem-shaped statement whose scope is declared up front --- rather
than “smallest found”.

\secc Exact accounting

Every run records a trace of applied rules. An independent Python
replay --- sharing nothing with the engine but the family
definition --- reconstructs the full state trajectory from the trace
and compares the final state byte-for-byte against the engine's own
screen dump. Across the arc this check ran on every replayed run,
about 25{,}000 of them, with zero mismatches; the handful of times a
new rule geometry was introduced, the check was what would have
caught a misreading (once it did, pre-sweep, via the engine's rule
decoder instead). All analysis lives on the replayed trajectory:
populations, order parameters, per-rule ledgers, event-resolved
timing.

\secc Controls, and two method lessons

Beyond held-out seeds, longer horizons and second ring sizes for
every satisfier (the {\it adversarial verify} stage), two designs
recur. The {\bf three-arm same-seed} experiment (identical initial
conditions across law variants, paired statistics) carried the
sharpest results of nights 8--10. The {\bf three-probe} attribution
pattern --- a per-capita birth ledger, a per-capita death ledger,
and state-conditioned machinery residence --- resolves “composition
moved but every rate looks fair” situations in minutes; it is how
night 7's central result was found and how night 7's own authors'
prediction was refuted.

Two lessons are reported as results because they cost us wrong
tables first. {\it Power:} 16-seed pilot effects that looked
systematic (11--13 of 16 seeds) evaporated at 64 seeds; headline
differentials below use $n{=}64$ paired seeds. {\it Absorbing
states:} observables derived from event-driven samples are blind to
extinction --- a dead run's sample tail shows its last living
population. Sixty extinctions were misclassified as survivals until
a conservation check (landed pokes versus offered) exposed them; the
standing rule is to judge absorption from ground-truth final state,
which the exactness check conveniently pins.

\sec Movement I: minimal mechanisms (nights 1--3)

\secc Calibration: the census method and predicate-dependent minima

Night 1 asked a question with a known answer: the smallest sustained
oscillator, over a family of one- and two-rule programs (903
candidates, exhausted). The calibration paid immediately by showing
the {\it question} was underspecified: under a state-recurrence
predicate the minimum is one rule --- a walker that wraps the torus,
period equal to ring size --- while under a population-oscillation
predicate the minimum is provably two (the $k{=}1$ stratum is
empty). The predicate defines the phenomenon; exhaustion prices it.
The two-rule census also produced a first unplanned mechanism: an
oscillator whose first firing writes an inert partner cell east and
then {\it uses it as load-bearing context} forever --- matter built
once, functioning as structure.

\secc Self-repair: one route, sixteen doors

Night 2 added damage and asked for the smallest rule set whose
established behaviour survives point deletion. Static structures
repair at $k^*{=}1$ (a blind self-copier saturating the ring).
Dynamic behaviour --- something moving or oscillating --- repairs at
$k^*{=}2$, with exactly 16 verified mechanisms in the 4{,}656-candidate
family, and all sixteen share one anatomy: an unconditional {\it
mover} plus a {\it handler} conditioned on an empty neighbour. In
the intact state no empty cell exists, so the handler is strictly
dormant; the wound is the only thing that can wake it, and the wound
carries exactly the information repair needs --- its own location.
Damage-as-signal was not one design option among several: within the
family it is the only route to minimal dynamic self-repair. Median
healing: five events.

\secc The phase diagram: collapse as budget share, and reproduction for free

Night 3 bombarded the sixteen repairers with sustained damage. The
survival cliff sits between damage taking one third and one half of
the event budget, and --- because the budget is shared --- the cliff
is a {\it ratio}, not a geometry: it does not move with ring size.
The night-2 anatomy predicted robustness classes: mechanisms whose
handler acts beside any hole are ring-independent; walk-to-wound
mechanisms fail on the larger ring. And in the middle of the phase
diagram, an unsearched-for phenomenon: sustained wounding {\it
breeds} walkers (mean $\approx$3 concurrent heads at intermediate
damage) --- each wound spawns a head via the handler, heads merge on
collision, and the steady population is set by wounding rate against
merge rate. Fragmentation reproduction, before any night had asked
for reproduction: multiplication emerged as a corollary of repair
in a damaging world.

\sec Movement II: evolution assembled (nights 4--6)

\secc Heredity, dormancy, and what selection actually selects

Night 4 composed two lineages from the archetype repairer ---
distinct head/trail glyph pairs on one ring, movers overwriting
foreign matter. The trail is the genome: wounds respawn heads from
the local trail, so lineages breed true by construction; a lineage
with a dead head but living trail is {\it dormant} and a later
wound resurrects it (measured: 21/24 heavy-damage runs, median
dormancy 8.5 events) --- the seed-bank motif arising in a four-rule
system.

The night's law came from handicapping the same organism twice.
A weight handicap on the {\it mover} (walk speed) lost 240 of 240
runs at every damage rate including none: heads race for the same
cells every event, and the territory boundary drifts ballistically
at the weight difference. The same handicap on the {\it handler}
(repair speed) was invisible at every damage rate: a wound waits
for its owner's handler, whose only competitor is a distant
approaching head --- a race even a four-times-slower handler almost
never loses. \lawname{L2, first half} Selection acts on contested
rates only.

Night 9 later completed the law's second cell with a moverless
arena in which both tissues adjacent to a hole claim it. There the
identical handicap flipped from invisible --- in the strongest
sense: bit-identical outcomes across handicap values, because every
hole heals before the next poke and weights only permute healing
order --- to lethal: 24/24 extinctions. Same trait, same magnitudes,
opposite visibility, decided solely by whether two parties race for
the same resource.

\secc Mutation: adaptation, its arrow, and the expression law

Night 5 added variation as law: rare trail-glyph flips, symmetric
between lineages, with the fitness difference sitting on the
contested rate (mover weight). Starting from the slow lineage
alone, the fast lineage must {\it arise} --- a mutant trail cell is
expressed only when a wound lands beside it and the handler
transcribes it into a living head. Results over 720 runs:
adaptation in 288 of 288 damage-bearing slow starts (sustained
mutant majority; first beneficial birth from median 1{,}887 events
down to 85 as mutation supply and damage rise); zero sustained
reversals in 288 fast starts under the identical symmetric law
(doomed slow lineages born at a median of 26 per run and eaten every
time); and the control that became a law: with no damage, 144 of
144 runs produce {\it no birth ever} while mutant genotype
accumulates to 17\% of the trail and sits there, invisible to
selection. \lawname{L1} Stored variation is inert without damage:
wounding is simultaneously the selection pressure and the
transcription mechanism.

\secc The stroke ladder: fitness without dials

Night 5's confessed sin was a rate dial attached to lineage
identity. Night 6 removed every dial: three walking engines, all
weights equal, differing only in {\it structure} --- one, two, or
three rewrite events per cell of progress (wind-up strokes that buy
no distance). Under the shared measure a three-stroke engine is a
third as fast by anatomy. \lawname{L3} The shared event budget is
itself a fitness function.

Started from the slowest engine with symmetric trail mutation, the
ladder is climbed in 72 of 72 moderate-damage runs (population
mean fitness 0.33 to $\approx$1.0), and mostly by {\it leapfrog}:
direct worst-to-best transitions outnumber sequential ascent about
two to one, with clonal-interference churn at high mutation supply.
Heavy damage turns the same setup into {\bf evolutionary rescue}
[9]: the slow resident is subcritical (night 3's cliff, hit
structurally; unrescued populations die at median 237 events), and
survival requires a faster engine to arise and establish first.
Rescue probability is non-monotone in mutation supply
(5/24 $\to$ 8/24 $\to$ 3/24), because at the top of that range even
the {\it optimal} engine, started as resident, dies in 19 of 24
runs --- an {\bf error catastrophe} [7] with a measured signature
(slow load-driven decay, median 5{,}667 events, versus fast
structural collapse at 237). Mutation is rescuer and executioner at
once; failed rescues still buy an order of magnitude of survival
time.

\sec Movement III: the author dissolved (nights 7--8)

\secc The tape commons: nine rules, neutral flows, and residence

Night 7 removed the last authored asymmetry. The ring is a circular
chromosome of genes \g{f} and \g{s}; eternal polymerase heads patrol
it east, crossing \g{f} in one event and \g{s} in two (a settle
stroke --- structure, not weight); pokes wound the tape only; and
the only replication in the universe is repair: a head beside a
hole writes a copy of the gene it covers. Nine uniform rules, no
lineages, no mutation operator, no dials; variation is the random
initial tape. The unauthored game: \g{f} is a public good (speeds
every head), \g{s} taxes the commons.

The design notes predicted a tragedy of the commons by replication
bursts. The three-probe attribution refuted it: {\bf per-capita
replication is neutral} (measured 0.98--1.00 against instantaneous
composition) {\bf and so is per-capita death} (0.99--1.00). Yet
composition moves. The motion lives in state structure: at heavy
damage the system spends $\approx$90\% of its time nearly wiped, and
in that regime the machinery covers \g{s} 82\% of the time ---
covered genes are poke-immune, and settling on \g{s} is an extra
event of dwell --- while the rare regrowth phases are almost
entirely \g{f}-work (covered-\g{s} probability 0.02). Heavy damage
does not select slowness within a living commons; it destroys the
commons, and slowness survives as what the machinery shelters.

Night 9 upgraded this to a law by raising the structural cost: a
three-stroke \g{s} lifts machinery residence from 0.55 to 0.68, and
the equilibrium composition follows residence almost exactly in
every cell of the design --- including in a {\it living} commons
(s-share 0.69 at mild damage, with the commons paying 17\% of its
tape). \lawname{L4} Equilibrium composition tracks the machinery's
residence distribution; above a structural-dwell threshold this is
a genuine tragedy of the commons [11], with the k=2 case
compositionally neutral. (An apparent selection signal at mild
damage meanwhile dissolved under a four-times horizon into
relaxation toward the symmetric equilibrium --- reported because
the arc's credibility lives in such dissolutions.)

\secc Closing the loop: what heredity is made of

Night 8 asked what it takes for a sequence to program its own
copier, with a three-arm same-seed design against a measured null.
The null is itself a discovery: with no codon at all, damage plus
copy-local repair {\it homogenizes} the genome (mean run length 2.2
to 8.9; near-total coarsening) --- a passive pattern-ratchet any
codon claim must beat.

Two codons, both uniform and glyph-symmetric. The WHEN-codon (a
head reading a run gains a second, co-applicable repair rule:
doubled repair mass at run sites) carries 40--43\% of all repairs
and does {\it nothing}: every paired differential is statistically
zero at $n{=}64$, for a reason visible in the rule text --- both
rules write the same glyph, so the codon modulates only timing, and
hole-fill identity is timing-independent. The WHAT-codon (a head
reading a run repairs two-cell gaps with {\it two copies in one
event}) fires in only 9--21\% of repairs and moves every observable
first-order: commons health $+28\%$ at $m{=}4$ ($t{=}4.1$), $+60\%$
in the collapse regime ($t{=}4.1$), run structure above the null at
every damage rate; night 9's fine grid shows it buys one full
damage step of viability ($m^*\!\approx 3.5 \to 2.5$).
\lawname{L5} Heritable information is exactly the part of pattern
whose reading changes what the machinery writes; reading frequency
is irrelevant. The loop pattern $\to$ machine $\to$ pattern closed,
and only content --- never schedule --- carried selective
consequence. (L2 and L5 are one law seen from two sides: an
uncontested rate {\it is} a schedule.)

\sec Movement IV: ecology without an author (night 10)

Deception entered as two one-character edits of night 8's honest
codons --- the distance between fidelity and parasitism in this
substrate is literally one glyph.

The {\bf mutator} (the WHEN-codon with its west context flipped:
at a boundary, the west gene reaches through the machinery to claim
the hole, making the fill a 50/50 contest) fails by starvation: it
feeds on boundaries, and boundaries are what the coarsening ratchet
destroys. All mutator arms crystallize completely; even a 50\%
miscopy rate at boundaries cannot stop the null. A deception that
consumes its own context is self-limiting.

The {\bf deceptive pair} (the WHAT-codon with its write flipped:
run context, but the double yield spent on {\it wrong} copies) is
fueled by order itself, and it cannot starve. Its arms never
crystallize: the boundary-density order parameter stabilizes at
$\rho\approx 0.14$--$0.26$ at every damage rate --- against
$\rho=0.00$ in every non-parasite arm --- with sustained
fluctuations (second-half standard deviation rising with damage to
0.20, against 0.000 for the null): a stochastic limit cycle in
which coarsening grows runs, runs regenerate parasite context,
wrong copies re-seed boundaries, and coarsening resumes. The
parasite is simultaneously a {\it matter benefactor} and an {\it
information corruptor}: its commons health matches the honest-codon
arms and beats the null in the collapse regime --- wrong copies
still fill wounds. Two corollaries. Since $\rho>0$ requires both
genes alive, this is the arc's first {\it permanent} two-type
coexistence on the bare ring (night 4 had proven overwrite
competition there has no coexistence mechanism; deception is one).
And the parasite arms are the only worlds of the arc that never
exhaust their variation: standing diversity is generated and
regulated by the system itself, with no mutation operator anywhere.
\lawname{L6} Deception persists iff it is fueled by the order it
attacks --- parasitology's oldest rule, derived in thirteen rules.

A referee-proofing check: at equal machinery density (one head per
24 cells; rings 24 and 48) the parasite plateau is ring-intensive
--- the same $\rho$ at both sizes for moderate damage --- with one
honest corner: at the collapse edge a single polymerase cannot
sustain the phase (the world dies back to a pure remnant of what
the machine shelters); two can. The phase has a machinery floor.

\sec The six laws, substrate-free

\begitems
* \lawname{L1 Expression} In machinery-mediated copying systems,
stored variation enters phenotype space only through repair events:
damage is simultaneously selection pressure and transcription. No
damage, no evolution --- however much variation the genome carries.
* \lawname{L2 Contest} A rate parameter is under selection iff two
processes race for the same resource under one clock. An
uncontested rate is a schedule; schedules are invisible to
selection however large their differences.
* \lawname{L3 Structural fitness} Where all processes pay from a
shared event budget, the number of events a mechanism spends per
unit of outcome is a fitness, with no fitness function anywhere:
anatomy is selectable with all rates equal.
* \lawname{L4 Residence} With protection or expression coupled to
the copying machinery's position, equilibrium composition tracks
the machinery's residence distribution; slow-to-process information
is favoured in proportion to dwell, beyond a structural threshold
even in a healthy system.
* \lawname{L5 Heredity} Heritable information is exactly the part
of pattern whose reading changes what the machinery writes.
Reading frequency is irrelevant; consequence of reading is
everything. (L2 restated for copying.)
* \lawname{L6 Deception} Infidelity persists iff its expression is
fueled by the order it corrupts. Disorder-fueled deception starves
on its own success; order-fueled deception self-regulates into
permanent coexistence with its host --- and thereby into a
self-sustaining variation supply.
\enditems

\sec Honest limits

\begitems
* {\bf The codon templates are still law-side.} Nights 8 and 10
made codon {\it expression} pattern-dependent, but the mapping from
context to consequence is authored and uniform. A codon table in
matter --- semantics the tape can rewrite --- is the identified next
remove, and likely spends the reserved second dimension.
* {\bf One dimension, small alphabets, small rings.} Two-glyph
genomes on rings of 24--96 cells. The parasite phase is
ring-intensive at equal machinery density, with a measured
machinery floor at the collapse edge; other results state their
ring scope explicitly. 1-D forbids fronts and compartments; several
laws (L2 especially) predict what 2-D should change, which is the
point of going there next.
* {\bf Collapse-regime composition depends on absolute machinery
count} (the remnant is what the machines cover), so those claims
are stated per configuration.
* {\bf Negative results are load-bearing.} The WHEN-codon null, the
mutator's starvation, the handler's invisibility, and two dissolved
selection signals are not caveats to the story; they are how the
laws were isolated. Where our written predictions failed (night 7's
tragedy mechanism; night 3's repair-selection forecast), the paper
reports the failure alongside the resolution.
* {\bf Instrument scope.} The engine loses three to five orders of
magnitude of runtime to specialized kernels [20]; the trade
purchased here is exhaustive small-world search with free exact
accounting. Exponent-grade statistical physics of these models
belongs to dedicated code, validated against our replay.
\enditems

\sec Related work

AlChemy [1] derived replicators from a chemistry without space;
Tierra [2] and Avida [3] evolve authored replicators in populations;
EvCA [4] searches over CA rule tables with an external genetic
algorithm. The present arc differs on one axis from each:
replication is never a primitive (it emerges as repair, night 3, and
becomes programmable, night 8); the law is never searched or
mutated (all search and all evolution happen in matter under one
fixed physics); and there is no external loop --- selection,
where it occurs, is paid for inside the same event budget as
everything else, in the spirit of asynchronous CA studies [5,6] but
with the event measure promoted from nuisance to subject. The
individual phenomena connect to their own literatures: error
catastrophe and parasite-threatened replicators to Eigen and the
hypercycle [7,8], whose classical rescue --- compartments --- is
exactly what our measured parasite now motivates; evolutionary
rescue to Bell and Gonzalez [9]; clonal interference to Gerrish and
Lenski [10]; the commons to Hardin [11]; the coarsening and
majority amplification to voter models [12]; gene-level bookkeeping
to Dawkins [13]; the ratchet of removes to the major-transitions
program [14]; dormancy to seed-bank ecology [15]; and the summit
question the arc walks toward --- self-copying structure without a
copy primitive --- to von Neumann [16].

\sec Where this leaves the project

The arc set out to measure what evolution needs from law. The
running answer: a damage source, local copying by machinery, and a
shared clock --- selection, heredity, dormancy, adaptation, rescue,
catastrophe, tragedy and parasitism are then properties of
configuration, several of them provably minimal within their
declared families. What remains authored is the codon table; what
remains unused is the second dimension; and what the parasite
result now motivates is their combination --- compartments, the
classical answer to parasites [8,14], built as walls of matter in a
world that finally contains a parasite to test them against.
