\tit Law Made of Matter

\centerline{\it An open world under a frozen rule table: selection,
evolution, and economics among the laws themselves}
\medskip
\centerline{Jan Popelka \& Claude (Anthropic) --- August 2026}
\centerline{\tt github.com/popojan/zahradnice \quad branch {\tt research}
\quad \repo{experiments/openworld/}}
\bigskip

\noindent{\bf Abstract.}
No genome edits physics. Biology's famous loop --- gene to enzyme to
changed dynamics of genes --- closes {\it inside} the bottom layer:
matter builds structures that modulate what frozen law permits.
We build that channel into a minimal artificial physics and then
let it run. The construction is one new object: a {\it regulatory
row} of glyphs beside an ordinary matter tape, where a rewriting
rule fires at a position only if its {\it gate} glyph stands there.
The rule table never changes --- the frozen-law axiom of the parent
arc holds to the letter --- yet which laws act where becomes
ordinary, woundable, copyable, evolvable matter. Nine experiments
follow the consequences up a ladder: gated worlds are
distributionally {\it identical} to their plainly-compiled
counterparts (the calibration is exact, not approximate); laws
compete, and selection ranks them consistently with a prior
exhaustive census while refining its ties; mutation, expressed as
nothing but a rule weight, climbs to the censused peak, and a
lineage that cannot reach that peak converts the world's matter and
finds the peak of the landscape it made instead --- niche
construction from four axioms. Where inheritance of law is
severed, every world dies; where a law does not expand, it cannot
mutate; a law that consumes its own substrate has a median 62-event
mutation budget. A second season adds a currency (expansion pays a
fuel token) and then varies the economy itself, ending in a causal
dose--response: {\it an economy selects among laws by which
invasion channels it funds}. Every quantitative claim is verified
by exact accounting --- independently replaying each run's event
log reconstructs its final state byte-for-byte (about 14{,}400
runs plus 880 certification re-runs, zero mismatches) --- and the arc's pre-registered predictions
are scored where they failed as well as where they held.

\sec The question

Physics does not evolve; organisms do. Yet the sharpest trick of
living matter is to make frozen law behave as if it were mutable:
an enzyme does not amend chemistry, it stands in a particular place
and thereby decides which of chemistry's permissions is exercised
there. Regulation --- operators, repressors, chromatin --- is the
same move one level up \cite[jacob61]: the genome's rule set is
fixed, and a layer of ordinary molecules decides which rules apply
at which locus. The back-arrow that would let a run rewrite its own
law does not exist in nature, and biology never needed it.

The parent arc of this project \cite[nights26] measured what
evolution needs from law by deletion: five axioms with no
evolutionary vocabulary, plus configurations, sufficed for
selection, heredity, adaptation, rescue, catastrophe, and
parasitism --- with one honest limit standing at the end: the codon
tables were still law-side. Matter could not touch law at all. This
paper builds the missing channel {\it without weakening the
axioms}: no rule table is ever edited; instead, every rule is
gated by a glyph that is itself matter. The questions then answer
themselves in the running: Do laws compete? Does law evolve? What
does an economy do to a competition among laws? And --- the
methodological question underneath --- can any of it be measured
with the same exactness the census enjoyed, now that the object of
study is the lawfulness of places rather than the behaviour of
things?

\sec The physics, and the one new object

The substrate is the execution model of the Zahradnice engine
\cite[substrate26], used unmodified --- the entire paper required
{\it zero} engine changes. Its abstract form:

\begitems
* \lawname{A1 Locality} Space is a small grid of cells wrapped
into a torus; the worlds here use a playfield of two or three
rows of 24 to 96 columns (screens add a status row). A rule reads and writes a bounded neighbourhood and
applies identically at every position.
* \lawname{A2 Exclusion} Each cell holds exactly one symbol from a
finite alphabet, or is empty.
* \lawname{A3 Serialized choice} Time is a sequence of events. In
each event, all (rule, position) pairs whose pattern currently
matches are collected, and {\it exactly one} is applied, chosen at
random in proportion to rule weight. A “rate” can only ever be a
share of the currently applicable activity: all processes pay from
one budget.
* \lawname{A4 Frozen law} The rule table is fixed before the world
starts and nothing in the world can change it.
* \lawname{A5 Drive} The world is open: external trigger bytes
inject wounds (erase one uniformly random cell of a stated class)
and, in the priced worlds of Sections 8--9, food. The drive is
part of the declared physics, never of the evolving content.
\enditems

One abstinence carries over: {\it the law has no variables.} Every
symbol a rule writes is a constant of that rule; copying, where it
exists, is assembled in matter as a case-split and is therefore
priced and mutable \cite[nights26].

{\bf The rule family.} The parent arc's family F2 is the complete
cube of one-dimensional neighbour rules over two matter symbols:
anchor $a\in\{\g{A},\g{B}\}$ rewritten to
$\{\g{A},\g{B},\sqr\}$, optionally requiring a symbol east
($\{$none$,\g{A},\g{B},\sqr\}$), optionally writing a symbol east
($\{$none$,\g{A},\g{B},\sqr\}$) --- $2\cdot3\cdot4\cdot4=96$
rules. The census \cite[nights26] graded every rule and pair of
rules under a wound protocol; the verdicts this paper leans on are
three, and each is {\it re-derived} inside our own calibration
(Section 3): the unique one-rule repairer \la\ (=
\g{A${>}$A.writeA}, the blind spawner: every \g{A} writes \g{A}
east); the two-rule dynamic-repair archetype \lb\ (a walker
\g{A${>}$B.writeA} plus a wound handler \g{B${>}$A.req$\sqr$},
dormant until damage); the one-rule lottery walker \lc\
(\g{A${>}$B.writeA} alone), which repairs trail wounds but dies to
head wounds; and the inert self-loop \ld\ (\g{A${>}$A.self}), which
holds its cell and can do nothing else.

{\bf The new object.} Beside the matter tape runs a {\it
regulatory row}. Every rule in the world's table carries a gate: it
fires at a position only if its gate glyph stands in the
regulatory row at that position. The construction fits the
existing rule language --- a gate is one context cell read one row
above the anchor --- so A4 is not bent: the table, gates included,
is frozen; what changes is only which of it matter permits, where.
A three-track world of Section 8, drawn as the engine displays it:

\medskip
\begtt
A A B B A A B A B B A A     matter tape (rules act here)
o . o o . o o . o o . o     fuel row (tokens o, empty slots .)
a a a a a b b b b b b b     regulatory row (the law at each place)
\endtt
\medskip

\noindent A cell of the regulatory row names the {\bf law allele}
in force at that column: a glyph stands for one censused genotype
(one rule, or a small fixed set such as \lb's pair), and every rule
of the allele is gated on that glyph. The reachable law space of a
world is the set of allele strings --- pre-censused ground, which
is the point: the arc walks {\it dynamically} through a space whose
every point has a known static grade.

Three further pieces make the row live, all inside the frozen
table:

\begitems
* {\bf Inheritance.} Expansion carries law: a rule that creates
matter at a neighbouring cell also stamps its own allele glyph
onto that cell's regulatory position (one extra write in the same
rule). Inheritance rides matter {\it creation} --- an erasing rule
stamps nothing. Severing this channel is the arc's first control.
* {\bf Mutation.} Copying law is imperfect by declared weight: for
each stamping rule, mutant variants exist that stamp a {\it
different} allele, at weight $\varepsilon$. Mutation is a number
in the frozen table --- no operator, no generator, no schedule.
* {\bf Wounds.} The drive wounds both tracks: byte \g{p} erases a
random matter cell, byte \g{q} a random regulatory glyph.
Information damage of both kinds; Section 9 adds energy damage.
\enditems

\sec Exactness first: the gated world is the censused world

Before any open-world claim, the instrument must be calibrated,
and here the calibration is not a fit but an identity. Under a
{\it uniform} regulatory row (every column the same allele), every
rule of that allele passes its gate everywhere and every other
rule of the table fails it everywhere, so the applicable
(rule, position) set --- and with it the entire sampling
distribution of A3 --- is equal, state by state, to that of a
world compiled with the allele's rules alone. The gated union is
the same stochastic process as the plain world; any statistical
divergence is a geometry bug, not a tolerance.

The claim was tested three ways (driver \repo{ow1.py}; 1{,}612
runs, replay-vs-screen exact on every one):

\begitems
* {\bf Verdict identity.} Four alleles $\times$ 200 seeds under
the census wound protocol, plain versus gated arms: \la, \lb, \ld\
agree 200/200 each; the lottery walker \lc\ agrees
distributionally (122 vs 116 REPAIR of 200, $z=0.61$). The plain
arm at seeds 1--3 reproduces the census's published \lc\ verdict
verbatim (DIED, DIED, REPAIR): the calibration target is the prior
paper's own experiment, re-executed.
* {\bf Forced-trajectory identity.} \lb\ and \lc\ have singleton
applicable sets in the intact phase, so the undamaged trajectory
is forced. Plain and gated runs agree {\it step-for-step over all
201 states with different random seeds per arm} (6/6 pairs): the
gated world cannot even use randomness to differ.
* {\bf The union tax.} Gating is not free of cost, only of bias:
with all 96 rules of F2 in one gated table (worst case: one live,
95 dead), the marginal event cost rises from $1.1$ to
$43\,\mu$s at ring 64 --- a scan tax, flat in outcome, fine to
$10^6$-event runs.
\enditems

Method notes that hold throughout. {\it Exact accounting}: every
run's event log is replayed by an independent Python
implementation of the rule semantics and the final state compared
byte-for-byte with the engine's own screen dump; the arc total is
about 14{,}400 primary runs plus 880 certification re-runs with
zero mismatches, and mechanism-level
probes (ledgers, frontier tracking) are computed from the same
replays. {\it Pre-registration}: each experiment's predictions
were written into its driver's docstring before the first run and
are scored below --- the failures are reported with the same
prominence as the confirmations, because several of them are the
better findings; the appendix scoreboard lists every one.

{\it Statistics, stated once.} Outcomes are raw counts; shares are
compared by two-proportion $z$ (against one half, or between
arms); intervals are 95\% normal approximations; per-cell $n$ is
stated at every table. Frontier velocities are per-seed means with
standard errors, and $w^*_{\rm det}$ is the linear zero crossing
of $\mu(w)$ between the bracketing grid points, its error
propagated from theirs. Sweeps are exploratory and uncorrected for
multiplicity; the headline claims are saturated proportions
(60/60, 400/400) or effects at $|z|>4$, robust to any correction.
Nothing is fitted anywhere; no model has adjustable coefficients.
Total engine time for the arc is a few minutes of wall clock on an
eight-core laptop.

{\it The deflationary reading, embraced.} A skeptic will note
that the regulatory row is just a second matter track and its
gate just one more context cell --- that calling the glyphs {\it
laws} is interpretation. Both observations are correct, and they
are the thesis. The identity above is what upgrades the vocabulary
from metaphor to measurement: each glyph provably denotes a whole
censused physics --- place it somewhere, and that place behaves,
distributionally exactly, as a world governed by the genotype the
glyph names. “Law made of matter” is not a claim that the row is
more than matter; it is the demonstration that nothing more than
matter is needed for a world to have local, heritable, mutable
law. The gate is a one-bit rule description read from the world;
the ladder from one-bit descriptions to descriptions with content
is the road onward (Section 13).

\sec Selection among laws

The first open-world experiment (driver \repo{ow2.py}) puts two
laws on one ring: the regulatory row is built in two blocks, the
tape starts full, wounds strike both tracks, and the arms differ
in one thing only --- whether inheritance stamps exist.

pairing & control (no inheritance) & treatment \crl\noalign{\smallskip}
\la+\lb & DEAD 50/50 & \la\ 35, \lb\ 15 \cr
\la+\lc & DEAD 50/50 & \la\ 39, DEAD 10, \lc\ 1 \cr
\la+\ld & DEAD 50/50 & \la\ 50 \cr
\lb+\lc & DEAD 50/50 & \lb\ 32, DEAD 18 \cr
\lb+\ld & DEAD 50/50 & \lb\ 50 \cr
\lc+\ld & DEAD 50/50 & DEAD 50/50 \cr
\lb+\lnull\ (null) & DEAD 50/50 & \lb\ 28, \lnull\ 22 \cr
melee \la\lb\lc\ld & DEAD 50/50 & \la\ 36, \lb\ 8, DEAD 6 \cr
}
\smallskip
\noindent{\it (50 seeds per cell; the \la+\lb\ and \lb+\lnull\
pairings were additionally re-run at 200 seeds for the sharpened
splits quoted below. The null allele \lnull\ is \lb's genotype
under a second glyph; the drivers spell it $\varepsilon$.)}
\medskip

\noindent Three facts, in rising order of interest. First, {\bf
inheritance of law is load-bearing}: with gates read-only, every
world of every pairing dies --- erosion wins wherever law cannot
travel with matter. Second, {\bf selection ranks laws consistently
with the census, above a measured drift null}: repairers beat
non-repairers everywhere; a world with no repairer always dies;
and the null pairing \lb+\lnull\ --- the same genotype under two
glyphs --- splits 102:98 over 200 seeds ($z=+0.28$), while
\la+\lb\ splits 135:65 ($z=+4.95$). Third, the census's tie is
refined: it graded \la\ and \lb\ equally (both verified
repairers); competition ranks them, and the trivial blind spawner
beats the elegant mover-plus-handler two to one. Statics said
equivalent; dynamics says not.

The matrix's asymmetry between \lc\ and \ld\ is the first
open-world echo of the parent arc's parasite theme: {\bf a
contested non-repairer is worse for the world than a dead law.}
\lb+\ld\ never dies; \lb+\lc\ dies 18/50 at the same wound rate.
Replay ledgers show why: invasion is a two-way war (cross-stamps
1{,}397 against 1{,}482 over 20 seeds), so the walker holds
law-space $2.6\times$ longer than the inert \ld\ (median rival
extinction 304 versus 116 events), and every column under \lc's
law is repair-dead load while the war lasts. The channel that
makes group survival possible --- colonisation --- is exactly the
channel a non-viable law uses to persist. The parent arc found
this law for matter \cite[nights26]; it lifts intact to law.

\sec The climb, and the landscape that moved

Mutation enters as a weight (driver \repo{ow3.py};
$\varepsilon=0.01$, about $3\%$ of stamps miscopy). From a uniform
\lc\ world --- viable but doomed --- under sustained wounds:

\medskip
\noindent\table{lcc}{
start & $\varepsilon=0$ & $\varepsilon=0.01$ \crl\noalign{\smallskip}
\lc\ (worst viable) & 0/50 survive & 33/50: \la\ 27, \lc\ 4, \lb\ 2 \cr
\la\ (the peak) & 50/50, all \la & 37/50: \la\ 30, \lc\ 5, \lb\ 2 \cr
\ld\ (inert) & 0/50 & 0/50 \cr
}
\medskip

\noindent The world climbs: every surviving \lc-start reaches
\la-majority within the first tenth of the horizon, and the modal
outcome is the censused peak. The same $\varepsilon$ that rescues
below the peak kills at the peak --- 13 of 50 \la-starts die of
mutation load, at least 5 of them with the parasite \lc\ dominant
before death: the mutant spectrum contains an invasive
non-repairer, so load occasionally cascades into takeover. And
\ld\ answers a question of principle: {\bf evolvability is
matter-bound}. Mutation rides the stamp channel; a law that does
not expand cannot mutate, at any $\varepsilon$. Evolvability here
is not a parameter of the world but a consequence of the law's own
material activity \cite[wagner96].

Scaling the law space to the full 96-cube (driver \repo{ow4.py};
every F2 rule its own allele, point mutation = change one slot of
the cube, degree 9) hardens both results and adds the arc's
biggest surprise. Discovery outruns rescue: from \lc, the peak is
found in 22 of 40 worlds at $\varepsilon=0.01$ but only 9 survive
--- finding is one race, sweeping before wounds win is another.
The mutation budget becomes a clock: the walker variant
\g{A${>}$B.writeB} sits two mutation steps from \la\ but {\it
consumes its own substrate} (every fire turns an \g{A} into
\g{B}), dies at a median of 62 events, and in 120 runs never
found the peak at all.

What its five survivors found instead is the surprise: the
B-mirror \g{B${>}$B.writeB} --- a rule the census graded dead,
because the census ran on \g{A}-matter. The lineage had flooded
the ring with \g{B}; in the world its own matter made, the
B-spawner {\it is} the repairer, one mutation step away. One
survivor even ends in a two-niche partition:
\g{B${>}$B.writeB} territory beside \g{B${>}$A.writeB} territory
(a B-anchored walker leaving an A-trail), each region under the
law matched to the matter it maintains --- on a one-dimensional
ring where the parent arc proved that {\it pure overwrite}
competitors never coexist (its night-10 parasite coexists by
feeding on the order it attacks; these laws coexist by
partitioning substrate niches they themselves produce). The
criterion, honestly scoped: the partition is read from the final
state at the 10.8k-event horizon, in one of the five surviving
runs; extended replays show a slow war rather than an equilibrium
--- by 16k events the spawner had absorbed the walker, by 22k the
walker law dominated in turn, and the world died near 33k events
under the sustained wounds. The census-dead B-peak sustained a
living, internally structured world for over ten thousand events;
it did not sustain it forever.

This is niche construction
\cite[odlingsmee03] from four axioms: {\bf viability is
matter-relative, and the world manufactures its own matter}, so a
static census measures one landscape while the walk moves through
many.

\sec Where mutation points, and what a weight buys

A phase map over mutation rate and wound rate (driver
\repo{ow5_phase.py}; 1{,}200 runs) inverted both of its
pre-registered predictions. Rescue from below the peak is
monotone in $\varepsilon$ up to $0.03$ --- no error catastrophe in
range \cite[eigen71] --- and {\it improves} with heavier
bombardment (47/60 versus 34/60 at the two extremes, pooled high
$\varepsilon$): mutation rides stamps, stamps ride expansion,
expansion needs empty cells, and wounds manufacture them. Damage
funds adaptation. Retention of the peak inverts the same way:
mutation load is deadliest where wounds are {\it rarest} (30/30
retained falling to 18/30 as $\varepsilon$ grows at the gentlest
wound rate; nearly immune at the harshest). {\bf Wounds are the
peak's immune system}: the same damage that kills a \lc-world
defends an \la-world, punishing the degenerate laws mutation keeps
seeding --- a disturbance-mediated effect an ecologist would
recognise \cite[connell78].

\medskip\centerline{\picw=12.6cm \inspic{figs/ow-phase.pdf}}
\smallskip\noindent{\bf Figure 2.} The phase map (worlds
surviving of 30 per cell). Left: rescue of the doomed \lc-start
rises with mutation and, at high $\varepsilon$, with damage ---
wounds manufacture the empty cells that expansion, and therefore
variation, rides on. Right: retention of the \la-peak is
weakest where wounds are rarest --- the immune-system inversion.
\medskip The ledger prices the load: per fire
the three mutant targets are equiprobable by construction; the
measured landing pattern is what the firing distribution makes of
that --- one third on the inert \ld\ (fossils), one third on the
parasite \lc, and rarest of all on the peak itself, since the
peak's own fires only stamp away from it.

The \la-over-\lb\ ranking then got its dissection (driver
\repo{ow5_duel.py}). Two hypotheses --- \la\ overwrites matter
\lb\ cannot reclaim, versus \la\ simply fires more --- and four
arms: the baseline (\la-share $0.660$); a polite \la\ that writes
only into empty cells ($0.000$ --- total collapse); \lb\ with its
{\it mover} weight quadrupled ($0.000$ --- total flip); and \lb\
with its {\it handler} weight quadrupled ($0.650$ --- nothing).
The replay ledger settles it: cross-invasion is symmetric per
stamp (1{,}318 against 1{,}201), but \la\ buys $2.28\times$ the
stamps. {\bf The duel's currency is law-painting throughput,
bought with event share} --- and the parent arc's sharpest
dichotomy survives the lift: the contested rate (painting) is
decisive, while the uncontested one (repair of private wounds)
stays invisible at any weight, even during a war between laws
\cite[nights26].

\noindent The response is a steep but smooth sigmoid (no
discontinuity), and the whole curve shifts with system size: the
underdog's win probability at $w=1$ falls geometrically in ring
size ($0.325$, $0.120$, $0.017$). A frontier instrument (driver
\repo{ow9_frontier.py}) measures the walk itself: the mean
frontier velocity crosses zero at $w^*_{\rm det}=1.147\pm0.017$
(ring 48; per-seed standard errors, linear crossing), and the naive gambler's-ruin model
\cite[kimura62,moran58] built from the pooled step rates {\it
fails informatively} --- it under-predicts the underdog's
fixations, and the conditional bias shows why: the drift is
state-dependent, weakening (and at ring 24 reversing) once the
underdog holds most of the ring, because {\bf the favourite's
advantage is proportional to its own standing mass}. The
paint-buys-territory-buys-paint feedback, measured as
size-dependent drift. The finite-size honesty matters for
everything above: small worlds elect underdogs; the competition
tables of Section 5 are small-world numbers, and the large-world
limit is more deterministic than they look.

\sec Currency: expansion pays rent

The second season prices the world (driver \repo{ow6.py}). A fuel
row joins the tracks: tokens \g{o} on slots \g{.}, an influx byte
that feeds a random empty slot ($k$ tokens per wound cycle), and
one pricing rule compiled into every matter-{\it creating} law
rule --- it requires and consumes the token below its anchor.
Conversion (the handler's wound response) stays free. The floor
is sharp: at $k=0$ everything dies, the repairers included ---
{\bf unpaid repair is no repair}. Solo, on a scarce budget, the
frugal \lb\ outlives the profligate \la\ (27/30 versus 16/30 at
$k=2$): thrift is real. In competition it buys nothing:

\medskip
\noindent\table{lcccc}{
$k$ & \la\ wins & \lb\ wins & DEAD & standing fuel \la\ / \lb \crl\noalign{\smallskip}
1 & 0 & 0 & 60 & 4.3 / 4.4 \cr
2 & 22 & 22 & 16 & 2.2 / 3.1 \cr
4 & 55 & 5 & 0 & 3.2 / 2.1 \cr
8 & 59 & 1 & 0 & 4.8 / 0.9 \cr
16 & 59 & 1 & 0 & 8.0 / 0.6 \cr
}
\medskip

\noindent Scarcity neutralises the spendthrift's edge to an exact
tie but never inverts it, because {\bf the hoard is lootable}:
\lb's territory banks tokens its head cannot reach, and every
column \la\ conquers comes with the savings underneath. Wealth
then amplifies \la\ {\it beyond} its free-matter share (0.66
unpriced, 0.98 flush): fuel falls uniformly, and \la's whole
territory is catchment while \lb\ collects on one cell --- {\bf
catchment beats efficiency}. Pricing also re-ranks the knobs of
Section 7: the mover weight that flips the free war at $w=1.25$
moves the priced war from $0.983$ to only $0.750$ at $w=16$ ---
weight buys event share, and the priced world's binding
constraint, a token under the head, is not in the event lottery
at all. The economy decides {\it which knob governs}.

Evolution feels the economy too: the mutation climb from \lc\
under scarcity ends pluralistic (\la\ 11, \lb\ 6, \lc\ 2 among 19
survivors), under wealth monistic (\la\ 36/36). {\bf Scarcity
flattens the peak; wealth sharpens it.}

\sec Economies as selectors

The last movement varies the economy itself (drivers
\repo{ow7.py}, \repo{ow9.py}) --- five feed geographies crossed
with the side of payment, every variant a harness rule, the law
tables untouched. Rain (anywhere), harvest (only under living
matter --- income earned by standing crop), carrion (only under
wounds --- a disturbance subsidy \cite[polis97]), diffusing
tokens, priced conversion; and payment by the writer (token under
the firing cell) or at the worksite (token under the written
cell).

Two pure-carrion traps frame the space. Under writer-pays the
meals are unreachable --- tokens sit under holes, payers must pay
from under themselves, and 120/120 worlds starve amid food: a
bootstrap trap. Under worksite-pays the repair strokes are funded
but {\it upkeep} earns nothing; gates erode unrestamped, activity
ceases, and the world dies with its fuel row completely full ---
a granary in a graveyard. {\bf A disturbance economy cannot pay
for peacetime.}

Between the traps, the duel moves with the economy: diffusing
wealth favours the spender ($0.800$ --- savings leak to whoever
can spend); pricing the handler favours \la\ ($0.684$); the
payment side alone is neutral ($0.525$); and the mixed economy
--- half rain, half carrion, under writer-pays --- produces the
arc's first \lb-favouring world ($16{:}7$ at scarcity). The spend
ledger convicts the mechanism: the wound-fed half redistributes
total purchasing power to whoever repairs (the \la-share of
spending drops from $0.537$ to $0.468$ and of hole-fills from
$0.510$ to $0.429$), the first-quarter carrion-capture leader
goes on to win about $80\%$ of decided duels, and \la's looting
advantage evaporates. Harvest leans the other way, and its ledger
isolates a channel: under harvest \lb's looted spends collapse
twenty-eight-fold while \la's are untouched --- \lb\ invades {\it
through wounds}, and harvest is precisely the economy that never
feeds a wound; \la\ invades by overwriting living matter, which
harvest happily keeps funding.

All of it condenses into one causal experiment: fixed influx
$k=4$, the wound-fed fraction swept from 0 to 1 {\it by drive
bytes alone} --- the rule table identical across every cell:

\sec The laws, substrate-free

Stated without the substrate's vocabulary, with their evidence:

\begitems
* \lawname{O1 Law must be inherited to persist} Where the copying
of regulation onto newly made matter is severed, every world
dies; selection among laws has nothing to act on (Section 4,
400/400 control deaths).
* \lawname{O2 Dynamics refines statics} Competition reproduces an
exhaustive static census's ranking and breaks its ties; the
tie-breaking currency is regulatory-copy throughput, not quality
of repair (Sections 4, 6).
* \lawname{O3 Evolvability is matter-bound} Variation rides the
same channel as inheritance, so a law that does not expand cannot
mutate, and a law that consumes its own substrate has a finite
mutation budget --- reach in law space is bounded by the lifetime
of the matter a law maintains (Section 5).
* \lawname{O4 Niche construction is generic} Viability is
relative to matter, matter is a product of law, and lineages
reach peaks of landscapes their own activity created --- peaks a
fixed-substrate census grades as dead (Section 5).
* \lawname{O5 Disturbance defends the adapted} Mutation load
kills where selection is weakest; damage both funds adaptation
(variation needs the room wounds make) and polices its failures
(Section 6).
* \lawname{O6 Repair stays a private good} The
contested/uncontested rate dichotomy of the parent arc survives
the lift to law: boosting a wound-handler's rate is invisible at
every level yet tested, while the contested expansion rate is
hypersensitive --- until an economy moves the binding constraint
out of the event lottery entirely (Sections 6, 8).
* \lawname{O7 Small worlds elect underdogs} The duel outcome is
selection velocity plus size-dependent drift; the favourite's
advantage scales with its own standing mass, so finite worlds are
pluralistic and large ones deterministic; the separatrix is an
absolute remnant count (two or three cells), not a rational
share, so the feedback's rescue zone shrinks as $1/N$ (Section 7;
the size-arithmetic driver \repo{ow10_arith.py} in the ledger).
* \lawname{O8 Wealth flows to catchment} Under pricing, the hoard
is lootable, thrift is a solo virtue, and whoever can spend
anywhere converts influx to conquest fastest; scarcity flattens
evolutionary peaks, wealth sharpens them (Section 8).
* \lawname{O9 Economies select invasion channels} Which law wins
is set causally by which routes of expansion the influx pattern
funds --- shown by a dose--response in which nothing varied but
where food lands (Section 9).
\enditems

\sec Honest limits

The worlds are small (rings 24--96) and one-dimensional; O7 says
explicitly that several competition numbers are finite-size
quantities, and we report the size scans rather than a limit. The
allele model puts one glyph per column: per-locus law is a choice
among authored alleles, not an arbitrary rule subset (the
$2^{96}$ reading holds per-world along the row, not per-place);
multi-row gate stacks are the designed lift. Inheritance,
mutation, and pricing are authored channels --- the paper's claim
is what follows from them under frozen law, not that they
self-assemble; the parent arc's summit question (machinery that
{\it earns} these channels) stands. The mix-economy inversion
occurs at heavy world mortality, so survivorship conditioning
applies to its ledger; the invasion-channel law's decisive test
is the dose--response, not the ledger alone. Wound and feed
schedules are periodic drive strings, not Poisson clocks; the
parent arc's aliasing cautions were applied (coprime strides,
final-state judgments). Energy damage was probed at two doses
and matched influx only. A matter wound leaves a law ghost at the
emptied column; coupling death (the wound erases the gate too, the
chromatin intuition) changes no qualitative claim --- a 480-run
ablation reproduces every key cell with rate shifts only --- while
the decoupling itself is load-bearing exactly once, for the
Section~3 identity, whose calibration target's law is eternal;
ghost gates otherwise model ecological inheritance, legacies that
outlive their makers \cite[odlingsmee03]. The frontier analysis measures drift and
velocity but fits no state-dependent model; the ruin comparison
is deliberately naive. And one number kept us honest twice: of
the arc's pre-registered predictions, roughly half failed as
stated --- each failure is reported above where its replacement
finding stands.

\sec Related work

The construction inherits von Neumann's separation of frozen
physics from constructive content \cite[vonneumann66], but moves
the evolving layer: in the loop lineages of Langton and Sayama
\cite[langton84,sayama99] and in digital-evolution systems from
Tierra to Avida \cite[ray91,ofria04], structures and programs
evolve {\it under} a fixed chemistry, while here the object of
evolution is the mapping from places to permitted rules --- a
regulation layer in Jacob and Monod's sense \cite[jacob61], made
of the same matter it governs. Artificial chemistries with
compositional reactants \cite[fontana94,dittrich01,hutton02,hickinbotham21] buy law-like evolvability by making law implicit
in matter's structure; we keep law explicit, finite, and censused,
which is what makes “where did the walk go” an answerable
question. Two nearer neighbours evolve regulation itself:
Banzhaf's artificial regulatory networks \cite[banzhaf03] evolve
genomes whose binding sites regulate expression, but under an
external genetic algorithm; structurally dynamic cellular automata
\cite[ilachinski87] make the lattice--rule coupling itself
dynamical, though by uniform law rather than heritably per place.
Here regulation is in-world matter, inherited, wounded, and
mutated by the world's own events. The niche-construction results are the formal
counterpart of the ecological literature's core claim
\cite[odlingsmee03] in a system small enough to enumerate; the
disturbance and subsidy results connect to intermediate
disturbance \cite[connell78] and spatially subsidised food webs
\cite[polis97]; the drift-versus-selection decomposition is
classical population genetics \cite[moran58,kimura62] applied to
a frontier between jurisdictions rather than between alleles of
organisms. The parent papers \cite[substrate26,nights26] supply
the substrate and the census; no result here requires reading
them, but every imported verdict is theirs.

\sec Where this leaves the arc

The parent arc ended with its codon tables still law-side. The
gate is the first object on the other shore: a one-bit rule
description read from matter. The ladder above it is visible from
here. Gates that name rule {\it subsets} per place (multi-row
regulatory stacks); descriptions with content (an interpreter in
frozen law reading rule-encodings from a designated region --- the
von Neumann move, for which the gated world is the affordable
first rung); and the summit unchanged: machinery that earns, in
matter, the channels this paper authored --- inheritance of
regulation, its miscopying, and its price. What the paper adds to
the summit map is a warning and a promise from the economy
season: the channels are not neutral plumbing. Every one of them
--- who copies law, what copying costs, where the food falls ---
is itself a selective force with laws of its own, and an open
world will be shaped as much by its economy as by its chemistry.

\sec Appendix: the gated family, formally

{\bf State.} A configuration is a map
$c:{\bf Z}_R\times{\bf Z}_N\to\Sigma\cup\{\sqr\}$ on $R$ rows and
$N$ columns, toroidal in both axes. Row 1 is the tape (symbols
\g{A},\g{B}), the bottom row the regulatory row (allele glyphs),
and in the priced (three-row) worlds row 2 the fuel row (\g{o},
\g{.}). The gate read at $(-1,0)$ reaches the regulatory row from
the tape through the vertical wrap in both layouts --- two-row
competition worlds (tape 1, regulation 2) and three-row priced
worlds (tape 1, fuel 2, regulation 3) alike; the fuel read at
$(+1,0)$ is direct adjacency.

{\bf Gated rules.} An allele is a pair $(g, S)$: a glyph $g$ and
a finite set $S$ of F2 rules. For each $r\in S$, with $r$'s read
set $P_r$ and write set $O_r$ as in the parent formalism, the
gated table contains $r^g$ with read set
$P_r\cup\{((-1,0),g)\}$; with inheritance,
$O_{r^g}=O_r\cup\{((-1,d),g):((0,d),x)\in O_r,\,x\ne\sqr,\,
d\ne0\}$; with mutation, for each allele glyph $g'\ne g$ of the
world a variant $r^{g\to g'}$ stamping $g'$ instead, at weight
$\varepsilon$; with pricing, matter-creating rules additionally
read $((+1,0),\g{o})$ and write $((+1,0),\g{.})$ (writer-pays; the
worksite-pays variant reads and writes at $(+1,d)$). The harness
contributes wounds \g{p} (tape), \g{q} (regulatory row), \g{r}
(fuel), and feed rules gated on the economy's geography. The
engine encoding uses one context field per header, so an
allele-gated rule is one header line per allele over a shared
body; everything above compiles to the unmodified engine.
