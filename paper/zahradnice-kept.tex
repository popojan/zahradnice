\tit What Someone Can Keep

\centerline{\it Healing descriptions, drifting codes, outsourced
evolvability, and the re-earned unit of selection}
\medskip
\centerline{Jan Popelka \& Claude (Anthropic) --- August 2026}
\centerline{\tt github.com/popojan/zahradnice \quad branch {\tt research}

\bigskip

\noindent{\bf Abstract.}
This closes a five-paper arc. Its predecessors built, under a
frozen rule table, a world where law is matter (a regulatory row
gates which rules act where), then made the three channels that
evolve that law --- inheritance, mutation, price --- the work of
mortal machines. What remained authored was everything a machine
system needs {\it about itself}: descriptions could not heal, the
codon table was frozen, fidelity belonged to no lineage, and
nothing in the world was a unit. This paper removes those pieces
one at a time and reports what selection did with each. A
transcriptase gives descriptions two-copy redundancy --- and the
backup's first service is not repair but sacrifice: it absorbs
45\% of description wounds by existing, and at moderate damage the
world that lets its repair machinery die outlives the world that
maintains it. The codon table becomes a row of matter: an absent
code is zero translation, not slow; a mortal keeper encoded in the
description maintains it (revoking the last scaffolding); and in
a two-code mosaic the code fixes by pure drift, because a bad
code's costs are borne globally while a divided world's costs are
super-additive --- selection enforces that everyone speak one
language and does not care which. Fidelity made heritable in
machine lineages yields the arc's sharpest asymmetry: error's
costs land on the erring machine (it stands on what it ruined),
error's gifts outlive its visit (everyone grazes what it
planted) --- fidelity evolves upward easily and downward hardly.
Supplying the missing ingredient, linkage, in its two forms
separates them cleanly: recognition moves the mutator's share ten
standard errors; viscosity moves nothing, because it accelerates
drift as fast as selection. And walls --- full-column, zero new
rules --- finally make units: isolated faithful-genome sectors die
in 400 of 400 worlds (without machine error the rescue allele is
unreachable), sloppy islands buy survival with their own
mistakes, and in a walled world with migration the registered
prediction inverts: the fittest genome is the one that does not
mutate but lives downstream of one that does --- the discoverer
pays, the neighbour keeps. One law survives every mask the arc
put on it: {\it selection sees only what someone can keep.}
About 3{,}300 registered runs in this paper, every one replayed
byte-for-byte, zero mismatches, zero engine changes; predictions
scored where they failed --- and five of the seven failures are
this paper's named laws.

\sec The question

The fourth paper ended with machinery that earns its channels:
copiers, mortal and buildable, perform inheritance of law;
their errors are its mutation; their fuel and their queue time
are its price. But everything a machine system needs {\it about
itself} was still granted. The description that encodes machines
could not heal a wound. The codon table --- what a description's
symbols mean --- was frozen law. Fidelity was a property of
machine {\it types}, not of anything that breeds. And the world
had no individuals: no boundary anywhere collected a description,
its machinery, its law, and its matter into something that could
succeed or fail together --- the parent arc had in fact deleted
the organism deliberately, showing selection, heredity, and
parasitism run fine without one. An outside reading of the whole
arc put the standing instruction well: do not enrich the
substrate; remove the last hardcoded piece. This paper removes
four --- description repair, the code, lineage fidelity, and the
unit --- and the census family contributes a fifth removal in
kind (free compartments become paid walls). Each removal was
pre-registered, run, and scored; the failures were kept.

\sec The physics, restated once

The engine \cite[substrate26] is unchanged --- the arc's five
papers required zero engine modifications in total. Axioms:
locality on a toroidal grid; one symbol per cell; serialized
weight-proportional choice, one event at a time, every process
paying from one budget; a frozen rule table; an external drive of
wound and food bytes that is physics, never content. No rule has
variables: whatever copies, copies as an alphabet case-split,
which is why copying is priced and mutable. The earned family's
standing objects \cite[earned26]: matter creation DE-regulates
its target (a `?` gate gates nothing); a copier machine reads the
law below itself and stamps it east, mortal, excluded, mistaken
at its own declared rate; a translator machine reads a
description row and BUILDS machines from codons, itself included;
machinery runs on its own drive byte, its cadence the world's
machinery bandwidth. Every run is traced and independently
replayed to a byte-identical final screen; this paper adds about
3{,}300 such runs across seven experiment families.

\sec Descriptions that heal, and the decoy that outdoes them

EW-6 gives the description two-copy redundancy: a transcriptase
\Ph\ backs codons up to a spare row and restores a wounded code
cell from its backup; a new codon \g{t} encodes \Ph\ itself, so
the healer is written in the text it heals. Healing works
completely --- with the repair layer, description wounding at the
registered rates costs nothing (survival 35--40 of 100 across
wound doses against 39 unwounded), where the unhealed world's
description is annihilated and survival collapses to 13. But
three of the four registered predictions did not survive contact.
The repair layer's rent is paid in {\it genome length}: at the
original ring size the t-codons and \Ph\ displaced copier codons
below the wound economy's needs, and the smoke autopsy is this
paper's emblem --- a perfectly healed, two-copy description
standing over a dead world. The archive outlived the
civilization. And the registered self-reference prediction was
beaten by an unregistered mechanism: because wounds strike codon
glyphs wherever they stand, the backup row absorbs 45\% of all
description damage {\it by existing} (measured from traces:
110 of 246 wounds). This sacrificial shielding is free; active
repair pays rent in codon slots and lottery share; and at
moderate damage the world whose repair machinery decays
unreplaced (52/100) outlives the world that maintains it
(35/100). Redundancy shields before it repairs, and the shield
alone can be the better buy.

\sec The code as matter, its keeper, and the frozen accident

EW-7 moves the last authored mapping. Translation becomes gated
on a TABLE row: the frozen rule set holds only the {\it space} of
codon tables (two, in these worlds --- the working code and its
b/w swap), and matter holds the live one, column by column. There
is no global anything under locality: even a universal code must
be physically present at every column where it is used, and that
is the experiment. An intact uniform code is dynamically
invisible --- the world reproduces the earned-kernel numbers
exactly. An {\it absent} code performs zero constructions in 100
worlds: translation without a physically present mapping is not
slow, it is nothing. A wounded code dies column by column and
takes the world with it (2/100); a table-keeper machine \Xc\
maintains uniformity and restores 57/100 --- after one amendment
that is itself a law: at low transit priority the maintained arm
died five of five {\it with a perfectly repaired code}, because
slow immortal keepers are standing obstacles whose parking
columns are the translator's landing slots. Keepers must keep
moving; maintenance is priced in traffic.

EW-8 revokes the keeper's immortality (codon \g{x} encodes \Xc;
the closure holds under wounds, 62 vs 23 without it) and then
asks the mosaic question: two codes, half a ring each --- does
selection choose? The swapped code first shows what a code IS:
under it the same description staffs the world with ten
translators and three copiers, and every world dies {\it while
its machinery survives it} --- the mis-staffed shop runs on over
a dead tape. Description and code are co-adapted; content
matters through the dialect. But in the mosaic, the boundary
between codes drifts with no directional bias at all (final
working-code share $0.503\pm0.109$ over 100 seeds): machinery
mixes around the ring, so a bad code's cost lands on everyone,
and local selection has nothing to grip --- the code fixes by
drift, Crick's accident exhibited, in the neutral-theory sense
\cite[hubbell01,clifford73].

The registered
mosaic-lands-in-the-middle prediction failed downward: mixed
worlds survive at 13/100, far below the pure arms' average,
because matter death is contagious (erosion pursues across the
ring) while machinery benefit only diffuses. Dialect pollution is
super-additive --- which is itself a selection pressure, for code
{\it uniformity} rather than code content. The world insists that
everyone speak one language; drift decides which.

\sec Walls priced: the census family's fifth removal

Night 13, in the census family, replaced its free compartments
with walls that cost: a wall displaces a gene of capacity, taxes
every crossing (three events against one; up to 15\% of a run's
whole budget), and blocks every pattern-window rule across it. Its
predecessor had shown free compartments never suppress the
order-fueled parasite and at collapse feed it. Paid walls end the
argument by changing its terms: the parasite's ACTIVITY falls
monotonically with wall count to exactly zero --- at sector width
two, the deceptive pattern (same-glyph west plus a double gap)
physically does not fit between walls. Containment succeeds not
by partitioning populations but by denying the parasite's niche
the room to exist; at collapse the old inversion reverses
($\rho$ $0.162\to0.000$, $t=-4.1$, where free rows gave $+0.054$).
And the cure is itemized: in healthy worlds walls are pure cost,
including a new mutational load --- a head exiting a wall onto a
wound repairs it blind, and wall-injected disorder almost exactly
replaces the parasite's own. The wall preserves the pattern, not
the matter; the medicine is mutagenic.

\sec Fidelity as a lineage trait, and the asymmetry

EW-9 makes fidelity heritable: faithful \Pc\ and sloppy \pc\
copiers breed true (a machine splits east into an empty slot),
and reproduction is gated on standing over living law, weighted
by that law's machine-nurture --- the load-bearing design choice,
because without it a machine's consequences are a commons and its
lineage invisible to selection. Two instrument findings were
caught and registered before the sweep: selection needs roughly
twenty mutation events per run to beat drift in these
populations; and the naive sloppy machine, carrying extra mutant
headers, MOVES more --- and under exclusion the restless queue
more and breed less, a mobility--fecundity confound that tilted
even the neutral arm until copy mass was normalized.

\medskip\centerline{\picw=11.2cm \inspic{figs/kept-linkage.pdf}}
\medskip

With the instrument clean, the neutral arm drifts at exactly
$0.499$; the deleterious arm selects the faithful lineage
($0.577$, and half the worlds die of mutation load, the lethal
end of mutagenesis \cite[bull07,eigen71]); and the beneficial arm
selects {\it nothing} ($0.492$), though the sloppy lineage's
errors planted forty percent of the ring with double-nurture
land. The asymmetry is structural. A miscopying machine advances
onto the cell it just stamped: its deleterious error is
delivered to itself, immediately, before mixing can socialize it.
Its beneficial error outlives its visit and feeds every machine
that drifts across it. {\bf Error's costs are private; error's
gifts are public} --- so fidelity evolves upward easily and
downward hardly, which is the substrate's derivation of why
selection for evolvability is indirect and weak
\cite[wagner96,gerrish98], separated from every confound by
construction.

\sec Linkage, decomposed

What real mutator dynamics have that EW-9's world lacks is
linkage: the mutator rides the genome its errors improve. EW-10
supplies linkage in its two separable forms. RECOGNITION (the
nurture bonus over mutant-planted land belongs to the sloppy
lineage alone --- authored, deliberately: the arm tests whether
linkage rescues second-order selection, not whether it
self-assembles) moves the lineage share ten standard errors: the
mutator takes a 79:21 standing majority. VISCOSITY (walk rates
cut tenfold, so machines stay with their plantings) moves
nothing --- and the mechanism is a law: slowing movement freed
lottery mass into demography, turnover rose eightfold, and
acceleration speeds drift exactly as fast as selection; in a
small one-dimensional world the lineages form solid blocks whose
boundaries random-walk to fixation first. Spatial linkage without
recognition is self-defeating at this scale --- a caveat for
spatial-rescue intuitions \cite[boerlijst91,takeuchi12]. The
strongest form ran too: from an all-faithful start with a rare
fidelity switch, the mutator lineage invades to majority under
recognition and stays minority without it ($z\approx8$ between
arms; the switch overshot its intended rate, so the contrast, not
the level, carries the claim).

\sec Walls are linkage: the unit, re-earned

EW-11 buys with geometry what EW-10 authored as recognition. A
full-column wall --- the glyph \g{|} on every row --- costs zero
new rules: the spawner's east variants match no wall, stamps
cannot cross, the translator can neither build onto nor land past
it, machines cannot walk through. A sector behind walls holds its
own description, machinery, law, and matter, and succeeds or
fails AS A UNIT. Sector genomes encode their copiers: codon \g{b}
builds faithful, codon \g{p} builds sloppy; the world's alleles
are a fragile walker and a robust repairer reachable only by
machine error.

Two pieces of physics arrived unbidden, both registered as
amendments when the smoke runs exposed them. Sealed walls
deadlock: an east-only machinery economy in a bounded sector is a
{\it terminal conveyor} --- machines make one transit, pile at
the wall, and the pile grows back over the codon build-sites
until translation dies. The ring's wrap had been a hidden axiom;
the fix is a rare machines-only hop (the genome and the law stay
home; machinery migrates like enzymes between protocells, and the
hop weight is the island-model dial). And isolated matter is a
metapopulation island: a fifteen-cell population under the wound
schedule dies absorbing --- there is no spontaneous generation
--- while the open ring shrugs off the same schedule by
recolonization rescue.

\medskip\centerline{\picw=11.2cm \inspic{figs/kept-units.pdf}}
\medskip

With the wound economy rescaled to island size, the verdicts.
In full isolation the faithful genome is a death sentence: 0 of
400 sectors, because without machine error the robust allele is
unreachable --- at the unit level, evolvability is the only exit.
Sloppy islands convert their own mistakes into rescue 64 of 400
times, and every first discovery in the entire sweep --- 300 of
300 --- originated in a sloppy-genome sector. The open world
socializes everything: both region types survive 200 of 200 with
identical matter and identical rescue-allele holdings; no unit,
nothing to compare. And the walled mixed world inverted the
registered prediction: faithful sectors OUTLIVE the sloppy
sectors that save them (37 vs 16 of 200, $z\approx3.1$) and end
holding MORE of the rescue allele than its discoverers (4.9 vs
3.8 gates). East-only hops deliver itinerant mutators downstream;
the visitor plants and moves on; faithful maintenance keeps the
gift without back-mutation churn, while the discoverer erodes its
own gains at \eps\ per restamp forever. This is the spatial form
of mutator hitchhiking and purge in asexual populations
\cite[gerrish98], derived: {\bf the discoverer pays, the
neighbour keeps.} The first thing units did in this substrate was
specialize --- a mutagenic minority servicing faithful
neighbours, a division of labour of the kind multilevel-selection
accounts reach for at the origin of the central dogma
\cite[takeuchi19,msz95].

\sec The laws

repair machinery (Section 3).

code death is per-column; universality is a maintained condition

* \lawname{K4 Codes fix by drift, and dialects cost

selection cannot see; a divided world is worse than either pure
one, because matter death is contagious while machinery benefit

which (Section 4).
* \lawname{K5 Error's costs are private, its gifts are public}
The erring machine stands on what it ruined and everyone grazes
what it planted: fidelity evolves upward easily, downward hardly
(Section 6).
* \lawname{K6 Recognition selects; viscosity drowns} Linkage by
kin-gating moves the mutator ten standard errors; linkage by

* \lawname{K7 Walls make units, and the wrap was an axiom} A

* \lawname{K8 Isolated matter dies absorbing; connection is

* \lawname{K9 In isolation, evolvability is the only exit}
Faithful islands: 0 of 400; sloppy islands buy survival with

* \lawname{K10 The discoverer pays, the neighbour keeps} With

\sec The through-line

One law wears every mask above and most of the previous papers':
{\bf selection sees only what someone can keep.} Machinery
funding is selected exactly where machinery is scarce, and
neutral where it saturates (paper four). A rescue allele's
advantage self-limits, because rescue restores the abundance that
made funding invisible. The codon table drifts because nobody
owns its consequences (K4). Fidelity falls only where its gifts
can be kept --- by recognition (K6), or by walls (K9) --- and
even then the keeping is contested: the best keeper of a gift is
a genome that does not churn it (K10). Containment works
precisely when it converts a public niche into no niche (K11).
The selfish-gene reading \cite[dawkins76] is usually told as a
story about replicators; this arc's version is a story about
{\it property}. What can be kept --- by standing on it, by
recognizing it, by walling it, by not mutating it away --- is
what selection can see; everything else is drift.

\sec The arc, closed

Five papers, one deletion ladder run in both directions. The
census arc \cite[nights26] subtracted ingredients --- fitness
functions, mutation operators, lineage identity, finally the
organism itself --- and found selection, heredity, repair, and
parasitism among the survivors. The gated worlds
\cite[openworld26] made law into matter and watched laws compete,
mutate, and buy invasion routes (laws O1--O9). The earned paper
\cite[earned26] moved the channels' exercise into mortal machines
(laws E1--E10). This paper moved in the machines' own furniture
--- healing, code, lineage fidelity, linkage, boundaries (laws
K1--K11) --- and the boundary returned what night seven had
deleted: the organism, now earned, priced, and immediately
specialized. The arc's ledger across its five papers: every
result under a byte-exact replay discipline, every prediction
registered before its run, every failure printed --- this paper
alone: eighteen held, seven failed as stated, two partial, and
five of the seven failures are named laws above.

\sec What remains authored

The honest inventory, in ascending order of depth. The
kin-recognition rule of EW-10 (its earned counterpart --- a
recognition that self-assembles --- is the sharpest open
revocation). The fidelity values and the two-point \Pc/\pc\
spectrum, with the fidelity switch's rate (mutation of the
mutation rate is exercised, not evolved). The machinery cadence
--- the world's machinery bandwidth is a drive dial, not an
evolved allocation. The codon-table space (two authored tables;
which is live, where, is matter --- what tables are POSSIBLE is
not). Placement semantics (a codon builds east of itself) and the
east-handedness of everything, which K7 showed is load-bearing.
The wall layout and its hop rate (walls are placed, not built by
lineages that pay for them --- the census family's night 13
priced standing walls; wall ownership is open on both families).
The seed machines: every world's first constructor is given, von
Neumann's irreducible starting capital \cite[vonneumann66]. And
the rule kinds themselves --- the frozen law that is the arc's
axiom, not its debt. Each earlier paper ended by naming the next
gift to revoke; this one ends by listing what is left, and the
list is short enough to read.

\sec Related work

The unit-of-selection results sit where the major-transitions
literature points \cite[msz95,szathmary89]: what changes at a
transition is what is copied together --- here measured as the
difference between a commons (EW-9), authored recognition
(EW-10), and bought walls (EW-11). The mutator dynamics recover
the asexual-population canon --- clonal interference and the
hitchhiking mutator \cite[gerrish98], lethal mutagenesis
\cite[bull07], the error threshold \cite[eigen71] --- as
consequences of one privacy asymmetry rather than as separate
phenomena. The code results give Crick's frozen accident a
mechanical reading in the neutral-theory register
\cite[hubbell01], with the mosaic boundary as a voter-model
interface \cite[clifford73]; the super-additive cost of division
is a selection pressure for universality that does not select
content, adjacent to the conflict account of the central dogma's
origin \cite[takeuchi19]. Spatial structure helps cooperation in
the replicator literature \cite[boerlijst91,takeuchi12]; K6 marks
where that intuition fails (viscosity that accelerates
demography accelerates drift), and K10 where it succeeds for the
wrong beneficiary. The healing results connect to stress-induced
mutagenesis \cite[galhardo07] through the earned paper's
repair-coupled errors, and the sacrificial-copy finding is
polyploidy's oldest argument stated as a price comparison.
Niche-construction language \cite[odlingsmee03] applies to the
planted pasture literally --- and K5 explains why constructing a
niche is no way to own one.
